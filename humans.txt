  v1.0  Antenna      2026-03-15
        First stable public version with four recurring editorial voices.

  v2.x  Fermentation  2026-03
        Editorial, source, accessibility, linking, and site-reliability work.

  v3.0  Aperture      2026-06-13
        Pixel Nova's grounding strengthened through Deaf art, language,
        translation, timing, and visual culture.

  v3.1  Aperture      2026-08-07
        Editorial review and factual grounding strengthened. Current standard:
        unsupported quotations, events, and biographical details are not
        permitted. Earlier work is reviewed against this standard, not
        assumed to already meet it.

  v3.2  Resolution    2026-08-12
        Personas separated from ways of seeing and subject choice. Public
        explanation updated around the Mind Engine, authored constraints,
        fictional voices, and the rule that sources provide the factual floor.

  Current engine — changeover in progress from 2026-08-24
        A controlled changeover of article generation to a newer editorial
        process. Articles produced under it carry current-engine provenance:
        each one is written to a structure decided from its own source
        material, and every factual claim is checked against that source
        before the article can become a candidate for publication. Earlier
        articles were produced under previous editorial systems and should
        not be assumed to have passed safeguards that were introduced later.
        Earlier work is being reviewed and corrected where necessary; that
        review is not complete.

  v3.3  Epoch         2026-09-14
        The current publication separated from earlier work. What Crip Minds
        carries now is the work of the present editorial system; earlier
        essays remain published and readable as publication history.
