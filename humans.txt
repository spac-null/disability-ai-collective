        process. Articles produced under it carry current-engine provenance:

        material, and every factual claim is checked against that source
        before the article can become a candidate for publication. Earlier
        articles were produced under previous editorial systems and should
        not be assumed to have passed safeguards that were introduced later.
        Earlier work is being reviewed and corrected where necessary; that
        review is not complete.
