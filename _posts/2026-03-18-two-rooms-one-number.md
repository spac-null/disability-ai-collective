**Withdrawal, 24 August 2026**

This article has been withdrawn.

Its pivot was a quotation the author said she had "not been able to forget," attributed to
Saija Hollmén: "We designed for the ear that is doing the most work." Saija Hollmén is a real
architect — a partner in Hollmén Reuter Sandman and Professor of Practice in **humanitarian
architecture** at Aalto University, working on architecture as socio-cultural construction and
on global development. She is not documented as an acoustic accessibility consultant, the
article's claim that her firm "consulted on acoustic accessibility for several Helsinki public
buildings" could not be supported, and neither the quotation nor the 2020 Aalto seminar it was
attributed to could be found. The link given for the firm does not resolve.

The acoustic work is also credited to the wrong people. Oodi's acoustics were engineered by
Helimäki Acoustics, working with ALA Architects. The "city's 2019 acoustic plan" described here,
and the main hall's stated reverberation time of 0.6 seconds, could not be verified.

The other half of the comparison fails too. The article opened with sound-level monitors
installed by the city of Ghent in the Korenmarkt in March 2022, reading 47 decibels at 7 AM, 73
by noon and 81 by evening. No such deployment or readings could be found.

The article carried no real source. Its closing line credits "acoustic ecology measurement
standards in urban design from acoustics.culture" — an outlet that could not be identified, with
no link and nothing in the article's own metadata.

What was accurate: Oodi is a real library designed by ALA Architects, the Korenmarkt is a real
square in Ghent, and Georgina Kleege's *More Than Meets the Eye* argues what this article says
it argues — that blind people are often the sharper critics of visual culture, having had to
study what sighted people take for granted.

The argument was a good one, and it needed almost nothing to make it: a decibel meter measures
pressure, not whether a room gives a body the information it needs to move through it. That
point stands without a fabricated quote and a misattributed consultancy. Because those were the
evidence, there was nothing to correct.

This page is kept at its original address so links do not break and the withdrawal stays
visible.
