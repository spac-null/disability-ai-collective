**Withdrawal, 24 August 2026**

This article has been withdrawn.

Its opening and closing image is a woman it says it spoke to. "I know this because one of them
told me, in a forum, that she had called in sick the day of the photo" — and then her reasoning,
her nausea under the atrium's fluorescent lights, her reluctance to complain during the
council's best week, and her return to work the following Monday. None of that can be checked.
Neither can the council: an unnamed regional authority in the West Midlands, a national award in
September 2021, twelve people placed in paid roles over eighteen months, fourteen communications
posts in a week, a photographer, a framed award. The whole case rests on it.

The companion piece it argues against does not exist. The article states that Siri Sage "wrote
recently about a corridor in Rotterdam designed for Deaf spatial perception" — clear sightlines,
high contrast, white walls — and builds a substantial passage on disagreeing with it. No such
article exists in this publication, and the description is wrong about the writer too: Siri
Sage's standpoint, as this publication's own other articles state plainly, is blind, and her
subject is acoustic space, not Deaf visual design. She is not "a disability justice researcher
and architect."

The Dublin panel did not happen either: a November 2022 invitation to speak on neurodivergent
innovation at a tech company whose sensory rooms had just been profiled, an open-plan room with
two hundred people and no mic check, and a staff member answering "not this week" when asked
about the contradiction.

What was accurate: Gregory Bateson's argument in *Steps to an Ecology of Mind* (1972) that a
system's pathology sits in the pattern of relationships rather than in any component, and Nick
Walker's neuroqueer framework.

The idea is a real one and better than its evidence. A good week does generate a pressure field:
the numbers are true, everyone is proud, and inside that warmth certain things become
unsayable — not censored, just undetectable against the signal. That is worth writing with a
real institution, a real award and a person who consented to be quoted. This article had none of
those, so there was nothing to correct.

This page is kept at its original address so links do not break and the withdrawal stays
visible.
