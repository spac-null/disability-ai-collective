**Withdrawal, 24 August 2026**

This article has been withdrawn.

Its central claim was that an NHS integrated care board preparing for the WorkWell
expansion had built a referral database whose "barriers to engagement" dropdown offered
"lack of drive," "chaotic lifestyle" and "won't comply." The article then made that
dropdown its thesis: "The dropdown menu is the scheme."

The only source for it was an unnamed acquaintance said to work at an unidentified board.
There is no document, no screenshot, no publication and no board named. It cannot be
checked, and it is a damaging, specific and entirely checkable-sounding allegation about
real public bodies. Publishing it on that basis was wrong, and because it is the article's
thesis rather than a detail, there is nothing to correct here.

The article also opened with a dated personal account of trying to reach a council
employment scheme in 2019, up a lift that had been broken for six weeks. That is not
supported either.

The recruitment statistics the piece cited, drawn from Disability News Service's reporting
on the DWP WorkWell pilots and the IFF Research review, are real and were correctly
attributed. The argument that a scheme built around individual motivation will record
inaccessibility as non-compliance is a legitimate argument. It needed evidence, and this
article substituted an anonymous anecdote for it.

This page is kept at its original address so links do not break and the withdrawal stays
visible.
