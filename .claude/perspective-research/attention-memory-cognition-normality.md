## WHAT THIS CLUSTER IS FOR

ZENO's territory, which the library was thinner in than its reputation suggested: PR001 gave
five classification instruments, but nothing asked about **attention**, **memory** or
**what a routine is doing**, and nothing asked how a threshold moves over time.

The care taken here is against one specific failure: this is the cluster where
"neurodivergent people are better at X" would enter if it were going to. Every entry is
written so that the subject, not the category, supplies the answer.

### PR007-01 — WHAT LOOKS LIKE NOT PAYING ATTENTION

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: settings read specific observable behaviours as engagement or disengagement,
and the mapping is an assumption that is rarely written down and frequently wrong. What else
could produce the same behaviour is answerable from the setting's own records. Sharpens Axes
13 and 8. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A setting decides that certain visible behaviours mean attention and others
mean its absence — eye contact, stillness, note-taking, responsiveness. The mapping is
enforced through assessment and sanction while remaining unstated, and behaviours with
entirely different causes land in the same category.

**QUESTION.** What behaviour does this setting read as inattention or disengagement, and what
else could produce exactly that behaviour?

**CARRIERS.** Classroom engagement metrics. Driver monitoring systems. Courtroom demeanour
and its effect on outcomes. Remote-work activity monitoring. Eye-tracking in usability and
advertising research. Interview scoring rubrics. Clinical observation of "poor engagement".
Proctoring software.

**FALSE MOVE.** The superpower inversion — claiming the behaviour indicates *superior*
attention. That is the same error with the sign flipped and it is explicitly out of bounds.
The instrument asks what the **mapping assumes** and what else fits the observation; it does
not award attention to anyone.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the setting validated its behavioural proxy against an
independent measure of the thing it claims to detect, and that the proxy held.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 13 (gaze and social perception); Axis 8 (cognition and capacity).
Reaches Axis 12.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** Shared — ZENO on the threshold at which variation becomes deviation,
SIIRI on what the observing instrument can and cannot register.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR001-02 asks how a category enters a measurement. This asks about an
**observable behaviour being used as a proxy for an internal state** — a validation question
with its own literature and its own failures.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A driver alertness system triggering on a posture that is not
drowsiness. (2) A jury reading composure as indifference.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR007-02 — THE INTERRUPTION IS BUILT IN

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: environments interrupt as a matter of design, and the work done in them is
assumed to survive fragmentation. What that assumption costs is visible in error rates,
handover records and the informal countermeasures people build. Sharpens Axes 4 and 9. No
new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** An environment's routine interruptions are a design feature, not a failure —
and the work is planned as though it were resumable at no cost. The cost is paid in errors,
in unrecorded re-orientation time, and in the private workarounds people build to protect
continuity.

**QUESTION.** What does this environment interrupt as a matter of routine, and what work is
assumed to survive being interrupted?

**CARRIERS.** Hospital ward rounds and medication errors. Open-plan office design. Air
traffic control position relief. Emergency dispatch. Operating theatre protocols. Software
on-call rotation. Notification design. Any setting with a documented "sterile cockpit" rule,
or with none and an argument about whether it needs one.

**FALSE MOVE.** Treating interruption as inherently harmful. Some interruption is the job,
and some is a safety mechanism. The instrument asks what the environment **assumed was
resumable**, and needs the subject to supply evidence about a specific task.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the environment protects the task explicitly, or that the task
is genuinely interruption-tolerant by its own measured error data.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 4 (timing); Axis 9 (endurance and accumulated cost). Reaches Axis 8.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** PINA primarily — sequence, and who selected it.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR002-02 asks whose pace an interface assumes. This asks about
**fragmentation rather than speed**: a task can run at any pace and still be broken into
pieces, and the evidence is different.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) Pharmacy dispensing error rates against interruption frequency.
(2) A code review process whose quality varies with notification volume.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR007-03 — REMEMBERING LIVES OUTSIDE THE PERSON

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: memory in practice is distributed across notebooks, routines, objects, places
and other people, and systems that treat it as internal to an individual break in
characteristic ways when the external part is removed. Sharpens Axes 8 and 5. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** Remembering here is performed by a system that includes the person and
something outside them. Institutions credit and assess the internal part, design around it,
and then remove or replace the external part without recognising that they have removed part
of the memory.

**QUESTION.** What counts as remembering here, and which part of it lives outside the
person — in a notebook, a routine, an object, a place, or another person?

**CARRIERS.** Aviation and surgical checklists. Ship navigation practice. Care routines
holding a household's knowledge. Handover notes. Apprenticeship and tacit craft knowledge.
Memory palaces and mnemonic traditions. A long-serving employee whose departure breaks a
process. Digitisation projects replacing a physical arrangement that encoded knowledge.

**FALSE MOVE.** Reaching for the distributed-cognition vocabulary because it is available.
The subject must supply a **specific external component and a specific consequence of its
removal or replacement**. An entry that merely observes that people use tools has said
nothing.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the external component is redundant — removed or changed with
no effect — or that the institution already models the distributed system and plans for it.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 8 (cognition and capacity); Axis 5 (independence and assistance).
Reaches Axis 6.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** MIRA primarily — assistance hidden inside an action called
independent, here inside an action called remembering.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR003-03 asks what maintenance keeps an autonomy running. This asks the
same shape of question about **a cognitive function**, where the carriers, the evidence and
the failure modes are entirely different.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A library reclassification that destroyed the shelf adjacency
researchers navigated by. (2) An outage where the runbook existed only in one person's
habits.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR007-04 — THE ASSESSMENT MEASURES THE ASSESSMENT

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: a result produced under test conditions is partly a property of those
conditions, and the gap between tested and everyday performance is documented in many fields
without changing what the score is used for. Sharpens Axis 12. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** An assessment creates a situation — timing, observation, novelty, stakes,
sensory environment — and measures performance inside it. Where the situation itself shapes
the result, the score describes the situation as much as the person, and is nonetheless used
as though it described only the person.

**QUESTION.** How much of this assessment's result is produced by the conditions of the
assessment itself, and where does the field record the gap between tested and everyday
performance?

**CARRIERS.** Cognitive and neuropsychological testing against everyday function.
Examination performance against coursework. Job interviews against probation outcomes.
Capability and benefits assessments. Laboratory versus field performance in ergonomics.
Clinical gait or speech assessment in a clinic room. Driving tests. Any instrument with a
published ecological-validity literature.

**FALSE MOVE.** Concluding the assessment is worthless, or that the person's "real" ability
is higher. The instrument asks what the **conditions contribute** and whether the field
acknowledges it — not what the true score would be.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the assessment has been validated against everyday performance
and the correlation holds, or that its conditions are deliberately part of what is being
measured and it is used accordingly.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 12 (what a system can register). Reaches Axis 8 and Axis 1.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** SIIRI primarily — the instrument's range, and description as
non-neutral.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** The library now holds three measurement instruments and they divide
cleanly: **PR001-02** — the category shaping the instrument. **PR006-08** — the reference
sample it was calibrated on. **PR007-04** — the situation in which it is administered. Each
has different evidence and a different document.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A materials test whose standard conditions do not occur in service.
(2) An emergency drill whose announced timing changes the result it certifies.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR007-05 — THE ROUTINE IS DOING SOMETHING

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: repeated routines frequently accomplish something their official purpose does
not describe, and institutions that remove them on efficiency grounds discover the function
afterwards. Sharpens Axes 8 and 9. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A routine's stated purpose and its actual function come apart. The routine
regulates, prepares, marks a transition, distributes attention or holds a group in time —
none of which appears in the procedure. When it is optimised away, what it was doing becomes
visible in what stops working.

**QUESTION.** What does this repeated routine actually accomplish that its official purpose
does not describe, and what stopped working when it was removed?

**CARRIERS.** Pre-flight and pre-operative checks beyond their checking function. Shift
handover ritual. Sports and performance preparation routines. Liturgy and ceremony.
Self-regulatory repetitive movement. Daily standups. Factory line stoppage rituals. Any
procedure removed in an efficiency programme and quietly reinstated.

**FALSE MOVE.** Pattern-obsession honoured uncritically as epistemology, named in ZENO's
MUST NOT BECOME. Also: do not romanticise. Some routines are vestigial and removing them
costs nothing, which is a perfectly good finding.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the routine was removed with no downstream effect, or that its
function is already documented and designed for.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 8 (cognition and capacity); Axis 9 (pace and accumulated cost).
Reaches Axis 6.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily — is what looks like inefficiency actually the
mechanism, and can a system be valuable because it refuses to be optimised.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Nothing in the library asked what a repeated action is for. PR002-07 asks
about time outside the counted interval; this asks about **a counted, scheduled activity
whose purpose is misdescribed** — which is not uncounted at all.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A hospital's abandoned drug round ritual and subsequent error rate.
(2) A maintenance walkaround whose real output was informal fault reporting.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR007-06 — THE THRESHOLD MOVED

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: category boundaries are revised by identifiable committees at datable moments,
and the revision reassigns real people across the line without anything about them changing.
The revision is documented; its effects usually are not. Sharpens Axis 11. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A threshold is set, then moved. People on one side become people on the other
without having changed, entitlements and diagnoses follow the line, and the prevalence
statistics of the category shift for reasons internal to the committee.

**QUESTION.** When did the boundary of this category last move, who moved it, and what
happened to the people on either side of the old line?

**CARRIERS.** Diagnostic manual revisions. Blood pressure, cholesterol and glycaemic
thresholds. Benefit eligibility criteria. Poverty and low-income lines. Exam grade
boundaries. Species conservation status criteria. Air quality limits. Legal age thresholds.
Any criterion with a published revision history and a committee minute.

**FALSE MOVE.** Assuming every revision is capture or expansion for its own sake. Thresholds
move for good evidential reasons too. The instrument asks **who moved it, on what basis, and
what happened to the reassigned** — three separately evidenced things.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the revision was evidence-driven, transparent, and that the
reassigned population was tracked and provided for.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 11 (classification and diagnosis) primarily. Reaches Axes 1 and 12.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily — where the threshold falls, and what happens to what
falls outside it.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR001-03 asks what a classification licenses once applied. This asks
about **the boundary changing over time**, which produces a natural experiment: the same
person, differently classified, before and after a datable decision.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A diagnostic criterion revision and the prevalence change attributed
to it. (2) A grade boundary moved after an exam, and the cohort effect.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR007-07 — THE SEQUENCE, NOT THE SUBJECT, IS THE OBSTACLE

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: curricula and training pathways impose an order of learning that is often
historical or administrative rather than necessary, and people fail at the ordering rather
than at the material. Where the sequence has been varied, the comparison exists. Sharpens
Axes 8 and 10. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A field teaches its material in a particular order. The order is frequently
inherited rather than derived, and it makes certain prerequisites mandatory. People who
would have learned the subject fail at the sequence, and the failure is recorded as a
failure at the subject.

**QUESTION.** Why is this taught in this order, who decided it, and who fails at the point
where the sequence rather than the subject becomes the obstacle?

**CARRIERS.** Mathematics curricula and their prerequisite chains. Reading instruction
methods. Music pedagogy and notation-before-playing. Medical and legal training pathways.
Apprenticeship structures. Driving test components. Programming curricula. Any subject taught
in two demonstrably different orders with comparable outcomes.

**FALSE MOVE.** Claiming any order would do. Some prerequisites are genuinely structural. The
instrument requires evidence that **the order was chosen** — a committee, a textbook
tradition, an administrative constraint — and that an alternative exists or existed.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the sequence is derived from the material's actual dependency
structure, or that alternative orderings were tried and produced worse outcomes.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 8 (cognition and capacity); Axis 10 (who authored the conditions).
Reaches Axis 11.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily — a threshold that had to be drawn before anything could
be managed, here a prerequisite.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR003-06 asks how the route to a setting changes whether support is used.
This asks about **the ordering of a body of knowledge**, a different kind of route with
curriculum documents as its evidence.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A statistics course requiring calculus, against one that does not, with
comparable outcomes. (2) A trade qualification whose theory-first ordering follows funding
rules rather than practice.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR007-08 — THE CATEGORY ARRIVED LATE

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: when a classification is applied to a life or a record long after the events
it reinterprets, the earlier record does not change but its meaning and its uses do. Both the
original use and the reinterpretation are documented. Sharpens Axes 11 and 12. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A category applied retrospectively rewrites the meaning of an existing record
without altering a word of it. What the record was previously used to justify, and what it is
now used to explain, are both documented — and the difference between them is the finding.

**QUESTION.** What changes about an existing record when a category is applied to it late,
and what was that record being used for before?

**CARRIERS.** Adult diagnosis and the school and employment record it reinterprets.
Retrospective diagnosis of historical figures. Posthumous reattribution of authorship or
works. Re-examined institutional and asylum records. Cold case reinterpretation. Species
reclassification of museum specimens. Any record whose meaning changed while its content did
not.

**FALSE MOVE.** Retrospective diagnosis of historical figures presented as established. This
is a genuine carrier **only** where the dispute about the retrospective claim is the subject,
never where the article asserts the diagnosis. Also: any account of a late diagnosis must
centre the person, not their family's account of them.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the record's use and interpretation were unchanged by the
reclassification, or that the earlier reading has been retained alongside.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 11 (classification); Axis 12 (what a record holds). Reaches Axis 13.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily — a category invented at a time, applied backwards.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR006-04 asks how an uncorrectable record propagates forward. This asks
what happens when **the record stays fixed and the interpretive frame moves**, which is the
opposite direction and has different evidence.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A museum specimen reassigned to a new species and the distribution
claims resting on its old identification. (2) An accident report reread under a later safety
framework.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument. Retrospective-diagnosis carriers require unusual care —
see FALSE MOVE.

### PR007-10 — WHAT THE SYSTEM CALLS AN EDGE CASE

**STATUS:** **REJECTED** — owner authorisation batch 2026-09-13, NO_ACCESS_ORIGIN.
*Proposed question: "What does this system treat as an exception to be handled separately,
and what would it look like if that case were the design centre?" Rejected not as a duplicate
but as an access-origin generator: its most natural carriers are accommodation and special
provision routes, and it would have reliably commissioned stories whose central proposition
is that a separate route exists. Access may be evidence inside a story and may not be the
intellectual origin of one. PR004-05 already asks the legitimate version — when it doesn't
fit, which one changes — with the mechanism rather than the provision as its subject.*

### PR007-11 — THE SKILL WAS INVISIBLE UNTIL IT FAILED

**STATUS:** **REJECTED** — owner authorisation batch 2026-09-13, deduplication pass.
*Proposed question: "What expertise only becomes describable when it stops working, and who
was doing it unnoticed?" Rejected as a duplicate of PR003-03 (autonomy has a maintenance
layer) in cognitive vocabulary — the same invisible-until-absent mechanism. PR007-03 carries
the genuinely distinct component, which is that the invisible part may be external to the
person rather than merely unnoticed.*
