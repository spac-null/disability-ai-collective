### PR001-01 — NORMALITY AS AN OPERATION

**STATUS:** CANDIDATE — **held at candidate, owner review 2026-09-07.**
*Owner reason: valuable but broad, with a high risk of becoming a pre-decided
anti-normalisation reading. Needs another independent application or source before durable
promotion.*

**SOURCE BASIS:** *AUTONOMOUS* (Gevers), sections *Omgekeerde diagnose* and *Cognitive
Capitalism* — **verified**. The text states that algorithms implicitly set the norm, and
that as *normaliseringsmachines* they encode, monitor and standardise existence and
**narrow the bandwidth of what counts as normal**; AI is named an "over-the-top"
normalisation machine. Also verified: the exhibition refuses to label people neurodivergent
and refuses to measure differences "along a single yardstick". Adjacent, via Lewthwaite:
the book's own framing questions are *"what is normal?"* and *"who decides this?"*, and one
of its four sections is *Perfectibility and Perfection*.

**MECHANISM.** A norm may not merely describe an existing population. A metric, diagnosis,
protocol, optimisation system or technology can participate in *producing* the preferred
behaviour it presents itself as only measuring — and can narrow the range that counts as
ordinary while appearing to observe it.

**QUESTION.** Where does this system stop describing variation and start producing the
preferred version of it?

**CARRIERS.** A metric published before and after it became a target. A scoring or ranking
system with a feedback loop into the thing scored. A protocol revised toward its
easiest-to-measure cases. A benchmark that reshaped the field that adopted it. A default
setting that became the definition. A tolerance band that narrowed across revisions.

**FALSE MOVE.** Reaching for Goodhart's law as a finished answer. "A measure that becomes a
target ceases to be a good measure" is a slogan and explains nothing about a particular
system. The reading needs the located point where description turned productive, and what
happened there.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Extends Axis 1 (bodies & normal functioning) and Axis 12 (measurement).

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily; SHARED.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Axis 12 asks what an instrument can and cannot register — a question
about its *limits*. This adds a question about its *effects*, which the doctrine currently
asks nowhere.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A hospital ranked on a waiting-time metric that reshapes which
appointments are created. (2) A recommendation ranking that changes what gets made in order
to rank.

**BOUNDARY.** The source argues this at civilisational scale and with strong contemporary
political claims (named heads of state, tech oligarchy, "propaganda machine"). **Those
claims are not adopted and are not what makes this entry usable.** Only the mechanism
transfers, and only where a specific subject supplies evidence of it. The source also does
not establish that norms are *always* productive.

### PR001-02 — THE CATEGORY ENTERS THE MEASUREMENT

*Renamed 2026-09-07 (was "A NAME CAN BECOME A CAUSE") so the title matches the mechanism.
The reification claim that name once carried is now **PR001-09**, deliberately separated.*

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner review 2026-09-07.
*Owner reason: a precise transferable mechanism — classification uptake can enter the
measurement itself. Approval does not touch the boundary below: not every prevalence or
counting system has a looping effect.*

**SOURCE BASIS:** **Trudy Dehue, *Neurobiological Perfection / On the ADHD Movement*,
source 1 pp. 96–101 — owner-session verified against the primary text.** The essay discusses
**Ian Hacking's looping effect**: people may adopt or reject a classification, and the
availability and uptake of the category may affect what subsequent prevalence research
records. Corroborated independently at source 5 (Houtepen) quoting p. 99: in a country
saturated with ADHD websites, songs and dedicated products, a prevalence survey largely
measures **the popularity of the label**, and the label's availability combined with a social
emphasis on performing well leads many respondents to reinterpret their own disappointments
as a disorder.

**MECHANISM.** A measurement of how common a category is may partly measure **how available
and how taken-up the category has become**. Once a classification circulates, it supplies
people with a description of themselves they did not previously have; uptake and rejection
both feed back into what the next measurement finds, so incidence and circulation stop being
cleanly separable.

**QUESTION.** Does this figure measure only occurrence, or has circulation and uptake of the
category entered the thing being measured?

**CARRIERS.** A prevalence or incidence series spanning the period a term entered common
use. A self-report instrument for a category with public circulation. A definition's history
across successive editions of a manual or standard. A criteria list, and later the same term
used as the reason a case meets it. A report using a category to account for an outcome the
category was defined by. The move from "cases meeting criteria X" to "cases caused by X".

**FALSE MOVE.** Treating any category as fake because it was constructed. Constructed is not
arbitrary. The live doctrine already holds the correct form: categories are invented, which
does not make them wrong, it makes them political. Circularity must be shown in this
category's own usage.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Extends Axis 11 (classification & diagnosis).

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Axis 11 asks who invented a category and for whose convenience. It does
not ask whether the category has silently changed job from description to explanation.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A credit or risk band used to explain a default it was defined by.
(2) A type designation later used to account for the traits that defined it.

**BOUNDARY.** **Not all prevalence figures work this way.** The looping effect is
established here for a psychiatric classification with heavy public circulation; a measure of
something people cannot adopt, reject or self-describe with has no obvious loop, and applying
this to one would be a category error. Do not treat a rising figure as evidence of looping
without showing the uptake.

**This entry is the protocol's worked example.** It was opened from an owner paraphrase ("a
name can become a cause"), narrowed once a reviewer's quotation was read, and then split in
two once the primary text was verified — because Dehue makes **both** arguments and they are
not the same argument. The reification half is now PR001-09. Recording the sequence is the
point: two of the three states were wrong in a way that only the source could correct.

### PR001-03 — CLASSIFICATION CHANGES THE AVAILABLE ACTION

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner review 2026-09-07.
*Owner reason: classification can alter jurisdiction, intervention, entitlement, obligation
or authority. The durable question stays fixed on the concrete action unlocked or foreclosed
— never the abstract claim that "labels have power".*

**SOURCE BASIS:** **Repo-verified**, `automation/_corpus/insights.md`: a lifelong-invalidity
assessment in which a doctor asks an albino man when he "became" albino, and a workplace
doctor dismisses him citing albinism while admitting he did not know what albinism was —
authority and consequence attached to a name. **Partially verified** in *AUTONOMOUS*
(*Y'All Got ADHD*): naming can give "access to stories", and those who deviate are
medicalised, corrected or excluded — different institutional consequences following from the
designation. Live doctrine support: `WORTH_SYSTEM` treats material that converts a
classification into something a body must live with as the publication's central mode.

**Primary source, now verified:** **Trudy Dehue, *Neurobiological Perfection / On the ADHD
Movement*, source 1 pp. 96–101 — owner-session verified.** The essay states that introducing
a medical name places an observed pattern into a **different institutional framework**: a
medical diagnosis points toward treatment, and it creates **specific rights and obligations**.
That is this entry's mechanism stated in the primary text, and it upgrades the entry's basis
from corpus instances plus inference to a direct source statement.

**MECHANISM.** Naming a condition or situation does more than describe it. The name can
change which institution has jurisdiction, which interventions become available or
mandatory, which responsibilities attach, and which rights or obligations appear.
Classification is an act with consequences, not a label on a settled state.

**QUESTION.** What becomes possible the moment this situation receives this name — and to
whom?

**CARRIERS.** A jurisdictional trigger written into a rule. An entitlement consequence
attached to a designation. A statutory duty beginning at classification. An insurance,
licensing or procurement category. A form field whose value routes a case elsewhere. A
designation with an appeal route, or without one.

**FALSE MOVE.** "Labels have power." True, empty, universally applicable. The reading needs
the specific action the name unlocked or foreclosed, and the party it moved.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Extends Axis 11; touches Axis 5 (assistance) where the name gates
support.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily; MAYA where the consequence is provision or cost.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Axis 11 asks what a classification makes invisible. This asks what it
makes *possible* — the productive rather than the excluding side.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A weather event reclassified as a disaster, releasing funds and
transferring authority. (2) A software fault reclassified as a security incident, changing
disclosure duties, timelines and ownership.

**BOUNDARY.** **Do not universalise beyond classification systems where concrete
institutional consequences can be shown.** The source establishes the mechanism for medical
diagnosis, where treatment pathways, rights and obligations are the consequences; a
classification with no institutional apparatus behind it may change nothing at all. The
reading must name the actual consequence, not assert that one exists because a name was
applied. Corpus instances evidence the mechanism in individual documented cases and establish
no frequency.

### PR001-04 — CONTROLLED DIFFERENCE / UNCONTROLLED DIFFERENCE

**STATUS:** CANDIDATE — **held at candidate, owner review 2026-09-07.**
*Owner reason: a strong hypothesis, but presently too easy to turn into a generic market or
capitalism critique. Seek a second source, or a non-obvious subject where **both halves** can
actually be shown.*

**SOURCE BASIS:** *AUTONOMOUS*, *Omgekeerde diagnose* — **verified**: neurological
differences are rapidly detected and then either **reduced to faults or exploited as a new
raw material** (*grondstof*); the brain is described as a source of extraction, permanently
monitored and optimised. **Verified via Lewthwaite:** one of the book's four sections is
titled ***Norms and Difference as Commodities***, and the book covers "big pharma" and the
economics of the *niet-normaal*. **Verified via source 5, Ine Gevers, *Normaliteit op
recept*, p. 23**, in the sharpest form yet found: how can a nose correction or a full
make-over make someone *more* human today, while another person with a prosthesis is still
seen as *less* human? Two body modifications, opposite social readings — and the difference
is that one is chosen, purchased and elective. Source 5 also reports part 1 arguing that
perfection has moved from a dream of some to a required effort for many.

**MECHANISM.** Difference may be welcomed where it is selectable, marketable, brandable,
useful or under individual control, while variation that cannot be managed that way remains
pathologised or penalised. The same system can celebrate one difference and price another.

**QUESTION.** Which differences does this system reward when they can be selected or made
useful, and what happens to difference that cannot be managed that way?

**CARRIERS.** A customisation option set, and what it does not offer. An eligibility
boundary admitting chosen variation and excluding unchosen. A marketing register beside a
compliance register in the same organisation. A diversity statement beside a performance
standard. A pricing tier for a trait.

**FALSE MOVE.** Turning this into a general claim about markets or capitalism. Usable only
where the specific subject supplies **both halves** — the rewarded difference and the
penalised one — inside the same system.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Extends Axis 1 (normal functioning) and Axis 11 (classification).

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO; SHARED.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** A comparative question the doctrine lacks: not what a system excludes,
but what it *includes selectively*, and where the line between them falls.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A platform celebrating creator individuality while its moderation
rules penalise unclassifiable work. (2) An employer funding chosen skill variation while
managing unchosen variation through absence policy.

**BOUNDARY.** The source states this within an explicitly anti-capitalist frame. **The frame
is not adopted; the two-sided test is.** The source does not establish that every system
does this.

### PR001-05 — TECHNOLOGY PARTICIPATES IN MAKING ABILITY

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner review 2026-09-07.
*Owner reason: Moser supplies direct primary support for ability and disability being enacted
through arrangements of body, technology, practice and resources. Approved as an **EXTENSION
and sharpening of existing Axis 5**, not as a new doctrine axis. The reverse-solutionism
boundary below is preserved unchanged.*
*(History: NEEDS_RESEARCH → CANDIDATE earlier the same day, when Moser settled the research
gap this entry had named; then approved at owner review.)*

**SOURCE BASIS:** **Ingunn Moser, *Making and Unmaking Difference: Agency and Interferences
among Disability, Gender and Class*, source 1 pp. 258–265 — owner-session verified against
the primary text.** The essay treats disability and other differences as **enacted in
concrete practices and relations rather than as fixed properties**, with material
arrangements and technologies participating in those enactments. Two verified specifics: a
communication device changes **more than the narrow functional channel it supports**; and in
another case rehabilitation practices, a computer, writing, and a family's social, cultural
and financial resources **jointly contribute to "unmaking" disability and enacting ability**.

Prior partial support retained: *AUTONOMOUS* on context shaping the brain and on the social
model locating limitation in an environment that refuses to adapt; source 5 placing the essay
in part 3, *Mens & technologie*, built around the cyborg; Lewthwaite on the "'leaky'
distinction between human and machine". Those were the pointer; Moser is the answer.

**MECHANISM.** Technology may not merely compensate for a fixed body. Body, environment,
technology and social resources jointly *enact* ability or disability in concrete practices,
making the limitation a property of the whole arrangement rather than of the person inside
it. Note the plural: in the verified case it is not the device alone but the device together
with rehabilitation practice, a literacy, and family resources.

**QUESTION.** Is this technology compensating for a fixed limitation, or changing the
conditions under which the limitation exists?

*Question kept, deliberately.* Moser's enactment framing suggests a broader phrasing — "what
ability or disability is this whole arrangement producing?" — and it was **considered and not
adopted**, because it presupposes that the arrangement produces one and so cannot be answered
*nothing useful here*. The kept question is binary, discriminating, and refusable. Moser's
framing is recorded in the MECHANISM instead, which is where a presupposition is harmless.

**CARRIERS.** A design rationale stating what a device assumes about its user. A standard
revised after a technology became common. A task description rewritten around a tool. An
account of what the same person could do in two arrangements. A support model presuming the
device is present. **The non-device parts of an arrangement**: a training practice, an
acquired literacy, a family's or institution's resources, without which the device does
less.

**FALSE MOVE.** Solutionism in reverse — treating every limitation as merely arranged. Some
are not, and the live doctrine already refuses technology offered as a solution to a
political problem.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Extends Axis 5 (independence & assistance); touches Axes 1 and 12.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** MAYA primarily; ZENO where the arrangement is what gets measured.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Axis 5 asks what assistance was hidden inside an action. This asks the
prior question — whether the deficit that assistance answers is itself produced by the
arrangement.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A profession whose competence definition changed once a tool became
standard. (2) An examination whose difficulty is a property of its permitted equipment.

**BOUNDARY.** **Technology does not make every limitation relational, and this must never
become reverse solutionism.** Some limitations are not conditions of an arrangement, and the
live doctrine already refuses technology offered as a solution to a political problem; that
caution is unchanged by the upgrade and is the reason the entry is CANDIDATE rather than
promoted. Still overlaps Axis 5 substantially and **may belong as an extension of it rather
than as its own entry** — merge candidate at owner review.

### PR001-06 — SELF-NORMALISATION

**STATUS:** CANDIDATE — **held at candidate, owner review 2026-09-07.**
*Owner reason: likely overlaps existing Axis 13 ("the gaze migrates inward"). **Do not
promote a duplicate instrument merely because a later source gives it new vocabulary.**
Future task: decide whether it sharpens Axis 13 or is genuinely distinct.*

**SOURCE BASIS:** *AUTONOMOUS*, *Y'All Got ADHD* — **verified, and the source's own term.**
The text names *zelf-normalisatie* and *zelfkolonisatie* directly; describes a "soft
compulsion" to adapt and self-diagnose that slides "frictionlessly under the skin"; and
states the structural point that there is **no longer a single oppressive power that can be
named and unmasked** — everyone has become a subtle part of the same machine. It also notes
that deviation is expected to be mapped out in advance by the deviator. **Verified via
Lewthwaite**, quoting the book at p. 22: how eager we are "to impose restrictions upon
ourselves".

**MECHANISM.** An external norm can become a behaviour a person performs on themselves,
through tracking, scoring, self-categorisation, optimisation or algorithmic feedback.
Enforcement moves from institution to self, and becomes correspondingly hard to locate or
contest.

**QUESTION.** At what point does an external rule become something the user now performs on
themselves?

**CARRIERS.** A self-reporting instrument. A score visible to the person scored. A streak,
target or progress mechanic. A self-assessment required before an external one. A
recommendation pre-emptively complied with. An interface showing a person their own
deviation. A disclosure expected in advance.

**FALSE MOVE.** Importing the source's broad political claims wholesale. Extract only the
transferable mechanism — the transfer of enforcement to the self, at a locatable point.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Extends Axis 1, Axis 8 (cognition) and Axis 13 (gaze). **Note the
overlap:** Axis 13 already carries "the gaze migrates inward" from the interview corpus.
This is the same shape arriving instrumented — internalised through a measuring interface
rather than through social perception. **Check for redundancy before promoting; a merge into
Axis 13 may be the honest outcome.**

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily; SIIRI where the self-reading is a legibility problem.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Possibly only the instrumented variant of something Axis 13 has. That
variant is real and locatable, which is why it is CANDIDATE rather than dismissed.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A tracker whose user optimises for the metric unprompted. (2) A grant
applicant writing to the scoring rubric before anyone applies it.

**BOUNDARY.** The source's framing is polemical and contemporary-political. Not adopted.

### PR001-07 — LEGIBILITY ENABLES AND CONSTRAINS

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner review 2026-09-07.
*Owner reason: the strongest mechanism in PR001. Becoming legible can open recognition,
access and action while also fixing, narrowing or persisting a category. **Both halves are
kept, and whether legibility is good or bad is never pre-decided.** Approved as a sharpening
of Axes 11 and 12, not as a new axis.*

**SOURCE BASIS:** *AUTONOMOUS*, *Y'All Got ADHD* — **verified, and close to verbatim in
mechanism**: the paradox is called inescapable — naming **can liberate, reassure and give
access to stories, while self-categorisation equally limits and freezes** (*beperkt en
verstart*). **Repo-verified** in `automation/_corpus/insights.md`: a notary, visibly uneasy,
writing into a legal deed that a Deaf signer was capable of reading the translated language
and therefore understood what he signed — legibility manufactured so a transaction could
proceed, and fixed in a document. Live doctrine support: `FREEZE_SYSTEM` on what a record
cannot hold; `WORTH_SYSTEM` on what a system "has a field for".

**MECHANISM.** Becoming classifiable or visible to a system can create access, recognition
or rights, and can simultaneously narrow the person or situation into a fixed category. The
same act of becoming readable both opens and freezes. Neither half is optional.

**QUESTION.** What does becoming legible make possible here, and what does the same
legibility freeze?

**CARRIERS.** A registration or enrolment step and what it fixes. An attestation written so
a process could continue. A profile, record or identifier persisting past the situation that
created it. A category granting entitlement while foreclosing revision. A form whose answer
cannot later be changed.

**FALSE MOVE.** Deciding the answer in advance. Legibility read as pure surveillance harm,
or as pure recognition gain, is a position rather than a reading. **The answer is not
predetermined as positive or negative.**

**EXISTING AXIS.** Extends Axis 12 (measurement & what a system can register) and Axis 11;
touches Axis 10 (participation).

**MINDS SHARPENED.** SIIRI primarily — the *can it be perceived* half of Axis 12 with its
consequence attached; ZENO on the freezing.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Axis 12 asks whether a system can register something. It does not ask
what registration **costs** the thing registered. That second half is the addition, and it
is what the notary instance actually shows.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) An open-source project adopting a governance structure to receive
funding, and what that structure then makes unchangeable. (2) A language gaining an official
orthography — writable, teachable, fundable, and thereby fixed.

**BOUNDARY.** The source states the paradox about *self*-categorisation specifically; the
generalisation to any legibility event is ours and is not established by it. The strongest
candidate in the cluster, and still not promotable without that check.

### PR001-08 — EXPLANATION CAN BECOME CLOSURE

**STATUS:** CANDIDATE — **held at candidate, owner review 2026-09-07. Not rejected.**
*Owner reason: strongly supported for exhibition and catalogue mediation. Its transfer to
captions, AI summaries, legal explanations, dashboards and the rest is still a hypothesis
requiring additional mediation / access / translation research.*

**SOURCE BASIS:** **Lewthwaite review — verified, and the review's own formulation.** The
short texts accompanying each work "effectively identify/describe the work, but also fix it
in a certain discourse or context"; the danger is named that such a text acts "not as a
point of entry, but as a point of closure", which the reader is then obliged to resist. The
review also describes the book having to **remediate the artwork-as-experience** for a
reader who cannot have the embodied encounter, and records the reviewer's own inability to
know whether the supplied interpretation would have been hers. Adjacent live doctrine: the
PINA / Axis 3 position that a mediating layer changes what arrives while appearing to
preserve it.

**MECHANISM.** A mediating or explanatory layer can make something available while also
fixing the interpretation through which the recipient meets it. Access and framing arrive
together, and the framing is rarely presented as one.

**QUESTION.** Does this explanatory layer help someone enter the thing, or does it also
decide in advance what they are allowed to find there?

**CARRIERS.** A museum label or wall text. A caption or alt text. An AI-generated summary. A
dashboard's default view. A translation. A legal explanation given at the point of consent.
A model explanation offered alongside a decision. Documentation that determines which
analyses are attempted.

**FALSE MOVE.** Concluding that explanation is inherently closure. Explanation is frequently
what makes entry possible at all, and this publication's own commitment is *difficult ideas,
very easy reading*. The reading must show what **this** layer foreclosed. The review itself
models the honesty required: it declines to call the fixing a criticism, and notes that its
own review performs the same abstraction it describes.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Extends Axis 3 (communication & mediation) and Axis 12.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** PINA primarily; SIIRI on what description fixes.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Axis 3 asks what mediation changed in transit. This asks what mediation
**settled** — the interpretive authority the layer quietly exercised, rather than the
content it altered.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A dataset whose documentation determines which analyses are
attempted. (2) A court's plain-language summary that becomes the version the public argues
about.

**BOUNDARY.** The review observes this about exhibition catalogue texts. Whether it holds
for explanatory layers generally is our extension, unestablished. **Do not conflate with
ME001 below** — this is an instrument for reading subjects; that is a lesson about us.

### PR001-09 — THE NAME BECOMES THE EXPLANATION

*Created 2026-09-07. Carries the reification claim PR001-02 was originally opened with, now

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner review 2026-09-07.
*Owner reason: reification is distinct from category circulation — a descriptive category can
later be used as if it were the explanation for the observations used to construct it.
Preserved on approval: constructed does not mean false or arbitrary, and the circular
explanatory move must be shown in the subject's own evidence.*

**SOURCE BASIS:** **Trudy Dehue, *Neurobiological Perfection / On the ADHD Movement*,
source 1 pp. 96–101 — owner-session verified against the primary text.** The essay explicitly
**warns against reifying labels**, distinguishing a label or standardised description from
treating that label as though it were the **cause** of the behaviour or misery it describes.
This is a different claim from the looping effect in PR001-02: one is about what a
measurement measures, the other is about what an explanation explains.

**MECHANISM.** A category can begin as a shorthand for observed criteria and later be used as
though the category itself explains or causes the observations from which it was constructed.
The name stops summarising the evidence and starts standing behind it as its reason, which is
circular and usually invisible because the circle closes over years and across documents.

**QUESTION.** Is this label describing the pattern, or has it started being used as the
explanation for the pattern?

**CARRIERS.** A definition or criteria document, and later causal language using the same
term. A risk category. A diagnostic label. An administrative category. A model-generated
class. A report in which a category explains the very variables that defined it. The shift
from "meets criteria X" to "because of X".

**FALSE MOVE.** "Categories are invented, therefore unreal." **Constructed does not mean
arbitrary or false**, and the live doctrine already holds the correct form: categories are
invented, which does not make them wrong, it makes them political. The circular or
explanatory move has to be **demonstrated in the subject** — shown in its own documents,
across its own usage — not inferred from the fact that somebody once defined the term.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Extends Axis 11 (classification & diagnosis).

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Axis 11 asks who invented a category and for whose convenience. Neither
it nor PR001-02 asks whether the category has silently changed job from description to
explanation. That is a checkable move with a documentary trail.

**TRANSFER TEST.** Two non-disability domains. (1) **Credit and insurance:** a risk band
defined by default rates, later cited as the reason for a default — the band explaining the
data that constructed it. (2) **Software and security:** a severity class assigned from
observed impact, later used to explain why an incident mattered, so the classification
becomes the account of the harm rather than a summary of it. A third, if a check is wanted:
a school performance band defined by attainment and later offered as the cause of attainment.

**BOUNDARY.** **The primary source establishes this mechanism in psychiatric classification.**
Transfer anywhere else is a hypothesis to test, not an established result — and the transfer
tests above are illustrations of the shape, not findings. Nothing here licenses asserting
circularity in a subject; it licenses looking for it.

### ME001 — POLYPHONY WITHOUT CLOSURE

**STATUS:** META-EDITORIAL CANDIDATE

**SOURCE BASIS:** **Lewthwaite review — verified.** Fulya Erdemci (director, SKOR) summed
the exhibition up in two words, "plurality" and "polyphony" (Beurs van Berlage, 17 December
2009); the review finds the book marked by the same, "literally hundreds of voices, with a
powerful accumulative effect", and describes reading it as **dialogic rather than didactic**
— while naming the risk that an individual accompanying text becomes a point of closure.
**Reception contrast, verified in source 4:** the Volkskrant piece opens by dividing group
exhibitions into two kinds — those offering a broad theme with a wide range of perspectives,
and those where the curator puts a very clear personal vision on the theme — and files
*AUTONOMOUS* explicitly as **the second kind** (★★★★☆, Brutus Rotterdam, to 14 June;
Redactie, 25 March 2026). The same curator, read as plural in one project and as
vision-forward in another.

**THE LESSON, IF IT HOLDS.** A plural arrangement can defeat its own plurality the moment
one voice becomes the authoritative explanation. Applied here: **several minds should OPEN
candidate readings; Worth and evidence should select; a mind must not become an
authoritative explanation that closes the subject before research has tested it.**

Consistent with what the doctrine already says — §5 holds that convergence is an editorial
signal and never factual evidence, and that a mind's question carries no authority of its
own. What this adds is the failure mode stated positively: the risk is not that a mind is
wrong, but that it is **persuasive early**, and a persuasive early reading can close a
subject research had not yet opened.

**WHAT IT WOULD CHANGE.** Possibly nothing structural; possibly a note in §5. The
Volkskrant contrast suggests a second, sharper question for us: whether Crip Minds intends
to be the plural kind or the vision-forward kind, and whether the four minds are currently
producing plurality or a house voice with four labels. **Do not act on either until an owner
has reviewed it.**

### ME002 — PERSPECTIVE AS HYPOTHESIS GENERATION

**STATUS:** **OWNER-APPROVED META-EDITORIAL PRINCIPLE** — owner review 2026-09-07.

*Note on vocabulary: this is **not** a sixth research-entry status. The five statuses in
`README.md` (CANDIDATE, APPROVED_DURABLE, NEEDS_RESEARCH, REJECTED, RETIRED) are unchanged
and apply to research entries only. ME002 stays inside this meta-editorial section, is not

**PROVENANCE:** Owner framing, 2026-09-07 — found useful in discussion, recorded here rather
than added to doctrine.

**THE APPROVED PRINCIPLE, as owner-stated 2026-09-07.**

> Perspective knowledge does not tell an article what a subject means.
> It generates falsifiable hypotheses and questions about **where** a meaningful reading
> might exist.

**CONCEPTUAL SEQUENCE.**

```
INITIAL SUBJECT UNDERSTANDING
      → HYPOTHESIS
      → PREDICTED CARRIER / DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE
      → TARGETED RESEARCH TRIES TO SUPPORT OR KILL IT
      → LEDGER ALONE GRANTS FACTUAL PERMISSION
      → WORTH DECIDES
```

**A GOOD PERSPECTIVE HYPOTHESIS.**
- **can fail**
- **permits NOTHING HERE**
- **predicts where discriminating evidence may exist**
- **does not become factual evidence itself**
- **is not strengthened merely because several minds generated it**
- **does not lower Worth's evidentiary bar**

**DO NOT IMPLEMENT THIS YET.**

*(The pre-approval draft of this section stated the same chain in different words and is
superseded by the owner-stated version above. Two glosses from it are worth keeping: an entry
that cannot come back negative is not a hypothesis but a frame; and ending in NOTHING HERE is
a completed hypothesis, not a failed one.)*

**WHY IT MATTERS.** It names what this whole directory is *for*, and it gives a test for
whether an entry is written correctly: an entry that cannot generate a falsifiable hypothesis
has been written as doctrine, not as instrumentation. Read that way, the four-field format
(MECHANISM / QUESTION / CARRIERS / FALSE MOVE) is already a hypothesis template — mechanism
is the claim, question is the test, carriers are the predicted evidence, false move is the
disconfirming trap.

**EXPLICITLY NOT DESIGNED HERE, AND NOT AUTHORISED BY THIS APPROVAL.** Provider call count.
Prompt format. Scoring or ranking of hypotheses. Multi-agent implementation. Production
wiring. Any of that would be a system, and this is a framing. Approval of the principle is
not approval to build it.

**NOT REQUIRED TERMINOLOGY.** "Hypothesis generator" is a working phrase for internal use.
It is not proposed as public language and nothing depends on the term surviving.

### ST001 — DISABILITY PLACED ALONGSIDE OTHER MARGINALISED CATEGORIES

Both readable sources place disability beside other marginalised categories, in the standard
manner of their respective periods.

- **Lewthwaite / *Difference on Display* (2010):** the review reports the book ranging
  across "the biologizing of criminality", genetics, disability-selective abortion,
  biopower, and Haraway's cyborg — a diversity-discourse framing in which disability sits
  among other categories of difference. The reviewer opens by quoting Shakespeare and Watson
  calling disability "the quintessential postmodern concept" because it defies
  classification.
- ***AUTONOMOUS* (Gevers):** neurodivergence is explicitly located inside identity politics
  alongside "sexual preferences or gender (non)identities", with Gen Z and Alpha claiming
  DSM-5 labels; and the ordering being resisted is named as "neurotypical, neocolonial and
  patriarchal capitalism".

**Not adopted.** The live **NO PROXIES** doctrine is authoritative: another marginalised
group is not a stand-in, and a reading about one group's situation is not licensed by
another's. Borrowing one group's injustice to license a reading about another is the same
failure as an analogy, in better clothes.

**Why it is kept.** It is useful history about how the field argued in 2010 and how it
argues now, and it explains why the current doctrine holds this line rather than inheriting
the sources'. A future reader who encounters these framings in the primary book should find
this note rather than rediscover the question.

### ST002 — CONTEMPORARY POLITICAL CLAIMS IN *AUTONOMOUS*

*AUTONOMOUS* argues its case through named contemporary political figures, tech-oligarchy,
"cognitive capitalism", and a claim that social media has made everyone part of the same
propaganda machine.

**Not adopted.** These are the source's politics, not a mechanism, and importing them would
give Crip Minds a standing position on subjects it has not researched. Only the located
mechanisms (PR001-01, -04, -06) transfer, and only where a subject supplies its own
evidence. Recorded so that a future reader knows the omission was deliberate.
