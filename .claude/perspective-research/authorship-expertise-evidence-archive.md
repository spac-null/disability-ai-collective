## WHAT THIS CLUSTER IS FOR

Who is credited, who is believed, who is certified, and what the record kept. This is the
territory where disability has produced the sharpest ordinary-world questions — authorship
disputes, contested testimony, expertise held by people without the authority to state it —
and the library had almost nothing in it. PR003-08 owns *who defines the option set* and is
not re-asked.

### PR006-01 — WHO IS CREDITED WHEN THE WORK REQUIRED TWO PEOPLE

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: collaborative production is ordinary and single authorship is the exception,
but crediting conventions usually record one name and give the other contribution a lesser
word — assistant, facilitator, technician, carer. What that word is, and who chose it, is
documentable. Sharpens Axes 10 and 3. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** Where a work could not have existed without more than one person, a
convention decides whose name it carries. The other contribution is renamed into something
that sounds like support rather than authorship, and the renaming is where the decision
actually happens.

**QUESTION.** When this work needed more than one person to exist, whose name is on it, and
what was the other person's contribution called?

**CARRIERS.** Contested authorship where a work was produced through a communication
partner or facilitator. Amanuenses and dictated works. Studio assistants and fabricators.
Ghostwriting and "as told to". Laboratory technicians on papers. Translators on covers.
Interpreters in recorded proceedings. Access workers credited or uncredited on productions.
Any credit line with a hierarchy in it.

**FALSE MOVE.** Deciding the authorship dispute. Several of the strongest carriers here are
genuinely contested and some are contested on evidence that cuts against the appealing
reading. The instrument asks **what the crediting convention did and who set it**; it does
not adjudicate who authored what, and an entry that arrives at a verdict has overreached.
Also: do not profile a disabled person because they are disabled — the carrier is the credit
line, not the biography.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the crediting convention already names both contributions at
equal weight, or that the second contribution is genuinely mechanical by the field's own
documented standard and both parties say so.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 10 (participation and authorship); Axis 3 (mediation). Reaches
Axis 5.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** PINA primarily — authorship of the exchange, and a transmission layer
that is also a person.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** Nothing in the library asked about credit. PR004-02 asks what a mediator
changes; this asks what the mediator is **called in the record**, which is a different
artefact and is usually the only one that survives.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) Software written by a pair where the commit history and the paper's
author list disagree. (2) A field recording credited to the collector rather than the
performer.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument. Carriers in this territory include live disputes; treat
contested cases as contested.

### PR006-02 — THE EXPERT IS CERTIFIED, THE KNOWER IS NOT

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: ZENO's own further question — do they hold the knowledge, or only the
authority — made into a commissioning instrument. The split between knowing and being
licensed to state is documentable in who gets cited, consulted and paid. Sharpens Axes 11
and 8. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** Institutions certify authority, not knowledge. Where the two come apart, the
person who knows the thing is consulted informally and credited nowhere, while the person
licensed to state it is the one the record shows.

**QUESTION.** Who here holds the knowledge, who holds the authority to state it, and what
happens in the record when they are not the same person?

**CARRIERS.** Long-term patients and their own condition against clinical authority.
Technicians and operators against engineers. Nurses against consultants. Indigenous and
local ecological knowledge against survey science. Experienced tradespeople against
specification. Amateur naturalists and astronomers in published findings. Maintenance staff
who know how the building actually works. Any consultation whose acknowledgements differ
from its sources.

**FALSE MOVE.** Romanticising lay knowledge, and the mirror error of treating credentialed
expertise as automatically hollow. The instrument requires **a specific case where the two
diverged and something followed from it**, not a general suspicion of professions.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the institution has a route by which uncertified knowledge
enters the record with attribution, and that it is used.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 11 (who is authorised to apply a category); Axis 8 (what a system
takes competence to be). Reaches Axis 10.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily — certification, and a capacity the system cannot see.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR001-03 asks what a classification licenses someone to do. This asks
about **who is licensed to make the classification**, which is a prior and separately
documented question.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A safety concern raised by shift staff and recorded only once an
engineer restated it. (2) A vernacular plant name carrying distribution knowledge that the
formal survey lacked.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR006-03 — THE ARCHIVE KEPT WHAT WAS EASY TO KEEP

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: an archive's holdings are shaped by its storage, cataloguing and funding
constraints at least as much as by judgements of significance, and the constraint is
recorded in accession policy where the judgement usually is not. Sharpens Axis 12. No new
axis.*

**MECHANISM.** What survives in an archive is filtered by what the archive could physically
store, catalogue, fund and describe. That filter is systematic and it is not the same as a
judgement about importance — but later scholarship reads the holdings as if it were.

**QUESTION.** What did this archive's own storage, cataloguing and funding constraints
decide to preserve, and what kind of thing was systematically not kept?

**CARRIERS.** Institutional archives and their accession policies. Oral history programmes.
Ephemeral and performance art documentation. Born-digital material and format obsolescence.
Personal correspondence against official papers. Community archives outside institutions.
Broadcast archives wiped for tape reuse. Any collection whose catalogue predates its subject
having a name.

**FALSE MOVE.** Treating every gap as deliberate erasure. Some gaps are budget, some are
shelf depth, some are that nobody thought to ask. The instrument asks what the **constraint**
was and what shape of thing it excluded — and an entry that cannot name the constraint is
asserting suppression it has not shown.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the archive's accession policy is documented and its exclusions
were deliberate, stated and defensible on their own terms.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 12 (what a system can register and retain). Reaches Axis 10.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** SIIRI primarily — absent versus never-registerable, applied to a
holding rather than a reading.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR005-06 asks what a system records as silence. This asks what never
entered the system at all, for reasons recorded in a different document — accession and
disposal policy rather than a data dictionary.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A theatre archive holding programmes and set drawings but no
recordings, and what the field therefore says about that period's staging. (2) A hospital
records schedule whose retention period destroyed the longitudinal data a later inquiry
needed.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR006-04 — THE CASE FILE OUTLIVES THE PERSON

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: records written about a person, which that person never saw and could not
correct, propagate into later decisions with the authority of fact. The propagation path is
documentable. Sharpens Axes 12 and 11. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A record made about someone at one moment, for one purpose, by someone with
authority over them, becomes the input to decisions made years later by people who never met
them. The subject usually has no route to see it and no standing to correct it, and each
downstream use treats it as settled.

**QUESTION.** What does this record say about a person that they never saw, could not
correct, and that later decisions were nonetheless made from?

**CARRIERS.** Medical notes carried between services. School records and their effect on
later placement. Social work and safeguarding files. Credit and insurance files. Asylum and
immigration interview records. Personnel files and references. Institutional admission
records in history. Any record with a longer retention period than the decision that created
it.

**FALSE MOVE.** Assuming the record is wrong. The instrument works equally where the record
is accurate — the mechanism is **uncorrectable propagation**, not error. An entry that
depends on proving the record false has changed the subject.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the subject has an exercised route to see and contest the
record, or that downstream decisions demonstrably re-derive rather than inherit.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 12 (what a record holds); Axis 11 (classification). Reaches Axis 13.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** SIIRI primarily — the record as a produced representation with
consequences; ZENO where the record's content is a category.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR001-09 asks how a name comes to substitute for an explanation. This
asks how a **specific document about a specific person travels**, which is a different
evidentiary trail: retention schedules, disclosure rules and data-sharing agreements rather
than vocabulary.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A childhood note reappearing in an adult assessment. (2) An insurance
declaration shaping a claim decades later.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR006-05 — WHERE IN THE SEQUENCE WAS CONSULTATION PLACED

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: consultation held after a decision is fixed is a different act from
consultation that could change it, and the difference is visible in the project timeline
rather than in the consultation document. Sharpens Axes 10 and 4. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** Participation processes have a position in a sequence. Where the consultation
sits after the decision is materially settled — funded, contracted, designed — it can only
ratify. The consultation document reads the same either way; the timeline does not.

**QUESTION.** At what point in this process was the decision actually fixed, and where in
the sequence was consultation placed relative to that point?

**CARRIERS.** Planning and infrastructure consultations against procurement dates. Clinical
trial design and patient involvement. Product co-design programmes. Policy consultations
against drafting timelines. Curriculum reform. Standards committees. Any consultation whose
budget was approved before it opened.

**FALSE MOVE.** Treating all late consultation as bad faith. Some decisions genuinely must be
fixed before detail can be discussed. The instrument asks where the **fixing point** was and
whether the process was honest about it, not whether consultation was early.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the decision demonstrably changed as a result — a documented
reversal, a scope change, a rejected option — or that the process stated its own fixed points
in advance.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 10 (who authored the conditions); Axis 4 (sequence and timing).
Reaches Axis 14.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** Shared — MAYA on who authored conditions others were admitted to, PINA
on sequence and who selected it.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR003-08 asks who defined the option set. This asks **when** — a
timeline question with its own documents, answerable where the option-set question is not.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A hospital redesign consulted on after the contractor was appointed.
(2) A software deprecation "feedback period" opening after the removal was merged.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR006-06 — THE FIELD'S FOUNDING CASE WAS ONE PERSON

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: whole disciplines rest on a small number of intensively studied individual
cases, and what has been generalised from them frequently exceeds what the case showed. The
overreach is traceable in citation. Sharpens Axes 8 and 12. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A single case becomes a field's founding demonstration. Over time the case is
cited for propositions progressively further from what was observed, and the original
constraints — one person, one lesion, one context, one observer — stop travelling with it.

**QUESTION.** Which individual case does this field's standard account rest on, and what has
been generalised from it that the case itself never showed?

**CARRIERS.** Landmark neurological and memory cases. Index patients in medical genetics.
Founding cases in psychology teaching. Landmark legal judgments cited beyond their facts.
Type specimens standing for a species. A single archaeological site defining a period. Any
textbook chapter whose evidence is one name.

**FALSE MOVE.** Treating single-case evidence as worthless. Case studies are legitimate and
often irreplaceable. The instrument asks about the **gap between what was observed and what
is now cited**, which is a claim about citation practice and must be shown in citations.
Also: the person in a founding case is a person, not an illustration — do not write them as
one.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the field cites the case within its demonstrated scope, or that
the generalisation has independent multi-case support that the field actually relies on.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 8 (cognition and capacity); Axis 12. Reaches Axis 1 and Axis 11.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily — what a system decided a variation was, and how the
decision hardened.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR006-08 asks about a reference sample's composition. This asks about a
sample of **one**, where the problem is not who was in it but how far its conclusions have
travelled from it.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A materials failure mode named for one incident and applied to
structurally different cases. (2) A precedent cited for a proposition the judgment expressly
declined to decide.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR006-07 — THE CORRECTION NEVER TRAVELS AS FAR AS THE CLAIM

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: corrections propagate through narrower channels than the claims they correct,
so the corrected version and the original coexist with different audiences. The asymmetry is
measurable in distribution. Sharpens Axes 3 and 4. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A claim is distributed through a wide, fast channel. Its correction is
distributed through a narrow, slow one — a footnote, a notice, a database field nobody
re-reads. The two versions then coexist, and which one a person holds depends on which
channel reached them.

**QUESTION.** When this was corrected, who actually received the correction, and who is
still working from the original version?

**CARRIERS.** Journal retractions against continued citation. Product recalls and fleets
still in service. Updated clinical guidance against embedded protocols. Corrected statistics
against the headline figure. Errata in reference works. Map and gazetteer corrections
propagating into navigation data. Any correction whose distribution list is shorter than the
original's.

**FALSE MOVE.** Treating this as a misinformation story. The instrument is about
**structural asymmetry between channels**, not about belief, bad actors or credulity. An
entry that becomes a story about people believing wrong things has lost the mechanism.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the correction used the same channel with comparable reach, or
that downstream users re-query the authoritative source rather than holding a copy.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 3 (what arrives); Axis 4 (when). Reaches Axis 12.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** PINA primarily — information existing is not information arriving.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR002-01 asks whether an access arrangement expires. PR005-03 asks about
two outputs of different epistemic kind. This asks about **one claim and its later
correction reaching different populations**, which is a distribution question with its own
evidence.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A retracted paper's citation count after retraction. (2) A corrected
tide table still in a printed almanac in a wheelhouse.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR006-08 — THE STANDARD WAS WRITTEN FROM A SAMPLE

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: standards, norms and reference ranges are built from a specific reference
population whose composition is usually recorded somewhere and almost never consulted. The
sample, not the category, is this instrument's subject. Sharpens Axis 12. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A standard encodes a reference population. Whoever was in that sample defines
what counts as normal, correct or safe for everyone the standard is later applied to — and
the sample's composition sits in a technical annexe that users of the standard never open.

**QUESTION.** Whose bodies, voices, behaviour or conditions formed the reference sample this
standard was built from, where is that recorded, and who has been measured against it since?

**CARRIERS.** Anthropometric data behind vehicle and workplace standards. Crash test dummy
specifications. Clinical reference ranges. Drug trial populations. Speech and face
recognition training sets. Colour and illumination standards. Ergonomic and seating norms.
Sizing systems. Any standard with a dated annexe describing its source data.

**FALSE MOVE.** Asserting a harm the subject has not shown. A narrow sample is a documented
fact; a consequence is a separate claim requiring separate evidence. Also: the sample's
narrowness is frequently about sex, age, region or occupation — **not disability proxies**,
and NO PROXIES forbids importing one into the other.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the standard documents its sample, has been revised on that
basis, or is applied with stated population limits.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 12 (measurement) primarily; Axis 1 (what body is assumed). Reaches
Axis 11.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** ZENO primarily — what had to be defined as normal before anything could
be measured or managed.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR001-02 asks how a category enters a measurement instrument. This asks
about the **reference population** the instrument was calibrated on, which is a different
artefact, in a different document, with a date on it.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A pulse oximeter's calibration population and the conditions under
which its readings were later found to vary. (2) A room acoustics standard derived from one
concert hall.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument. NO PROXIES applies — see FALSE MOVE.
