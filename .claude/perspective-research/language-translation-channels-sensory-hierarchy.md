**THESE ARE DERIVED INSTRUMENTS, NOT A SOURCE-BACKED RESEARCH CLUSTER.** PR001–PR004 each

**What that does and does not cost.** APPROVED_DURABLE has only ever meant *trusted for

these instruments grant precisely as much factual authority as PR001–PR004 do, which is
none. What they lack relative to an earlier cluster is a demonstrated instance: PR002-07
can point at a paper where the mechanism was observed, and these point only at the axis
they sharpen. **The practical consequence is that the first several commissions from this
cluster are also its evidence.** A question here that repeatedly sends Research looking and
Research repeatedly finds nothing is not a question that needs defending; it is a question
to retire, and retiring it is a normal successful outcome, not a failure of the batch.

**No new axis.** Fifth consecutive cluster. All 32 instruments across PR005–PR008 sit
beneath the existing fourteen.

## WHAT THIS CLUSTER IS FOR

The transmission layer and the sensory hierarchy: what sits between a thing and the version
that arrives, which channel a field treats as the real one, which sense is permitted to
settle a question, and what a notation or a format decided before anyone used it.

PINA and SIIRI carry most of it. The existing library already owns two pieces of this
territory and they are **not** re-asked here: PR004-01 owns *which of two channels is
treated as the original*, and PR004-02 owns *the register a mediator assigns*. Everything
below had to be something neither of those already asks.

### PR005-01 — WHAT THE NOTATION CANNOT WRITE DOWN

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: every field that writes things down chose a symbol set, and the choice
excluded something. Where practitioners are trained from the notation rather than from the
practice, the excluded thing stops being transmitted and eventually stops being noticed.
Sharpens Axes 3 and 12. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A notation is a lossy encoding that presents itself as a complete record.
What it has no symbol for does not merely go unrecorded — it drops out of training, out of
analysis, and out of what the field can later say it knows.

**QUESTION.** What does this field's standard notation have no symbol for, and what happens
to that when practitioners are trained from the notation rather than from the practice?

**CARRIERS.** A musical score beside a performance tradition. Dance notation. A court
transcript beside the hearing. Chemical structure notation. Knitting and weaving patterns.
Chess notation. Clinical coding. A signed language written down. Any field with a
"standard" written form and an oral or embodied one.

**FALSE MOVE.** Treating every omission as a defect. Notations are supposed to abstract;
that is their use. The instrument asks what the abstraction cost **and who stopped being
able to do the excluded thing**, not whether abstraction occurred.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the notation does record it, or that practitioners reliably
acquire it by another route that the field maintains deliberately. Either closes it.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 3 (communication and mediation); Axis 12 (what a system can
register). Reaches Axis 2.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** PINA primarily — a format that flattens while appearing to preserve.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR004-01 asks which of two existing channels is treated as the
original. This asks a prior question: what the authoritative channel was **never able to
hold**, whichever one is treated as source of truth.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) Ornamentation in a performance tradition that the score cannot
specify, and what happened to it once conservatoire training became score-based. (2) Tone
of voice in a court transcript, when appellate review reads only the transcript.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above. No source-demonstrated instance.

### PR005-02 — WHO IS BLAMED WHEN A MEDIATED EXCHANGE FAILS

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: fault attribution in a mediated chain reveals what the institution actually
believes the mediator was — a neutral conduit, a professional, or a person with
discretion — and that belief is usually only visible when something goes wrong. Sharpens
Axis 3. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** Where a message passes through a person or a system before arriving, failure
forces an attribution: the speaker, the mediator, or the format. Which one an institution
reaches for shows what it had tacitly decided the mediating layer was, and that decision is
rarely written anywhere until it is needed.

**QUESTION.** When something goes wrong in a mediated exchange here, who is held
responsible — the original speaker, the person or system in the middle, or the format — and
what does that choice reveal about what the middle was assumed to be?

**CARRIERS.** A mistranslation in a legal proceeding. A machine-translation error in a
consequential document. An interpreted medical consultation. A press officer's briefing. A
relay chain in broadcasting or emergency dispatch. An automated transcription in evidence.
A subtitled statement that caused a diplomatic incident.

**FALSE MOVE.** Interpreter-as-villain, explicitly named in PINA's MUST NOT BECOME. The
instrument is about **where an institution puts fault**, not about mediators making errors.
An entry that becomes a story about a bad interpreter has failed.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the institution has an explicit, published, followed rule
about responsibility in the mediated chain, and that the outcome matched it. A settled
question is not this instrument's subject.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 3 primarily; reaches Axis 10 (who authored the terms) and Axis 13.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** PINA primarily.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR004-02 asks what register a mediator assigns while mediating. This
asks what the institution does **afterwards, when the mediation is contested** — a
different moment, different evidence, and usually a different record.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) An aviation incident where a readback error is attributed to
phraseology rather than to either speaker. (2) A financial misstatement attributed to a
reporting template.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR005-03 — A FAST ACCOUNT AND AN ACCURATE ONE ARE DIFFERENT KINDS OF CLAIM

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: this is the reviewed primary-derived seed already carried in the production
Perspective Library index (perspective_explorer.PERSPECTIVE_MATERIAL), promoted here to a
first-class commissioning instrument. Its point is that a fast provisional output and a slow
accurate one are two legitimate epistemic modes, not a good answer and a defective one.
Sharpens Axes 3, 4 and 12. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** Where a system produces a quick provisional account and a slower, more
accurate one about the same event, the two are different **kinds** of claim. If the system
does not mark which is which, whichever arrives first tends to become "the record" that the
accurate one must later displace, rather than simply complete.

**QUESTION.** When a system produces a fast provisional account and a slower accurate one
about the same event, does it mark which kind each is — or does whichever arrives first
become the record the other has to overturn?

**CARRIERS.** Real-time interpretation beside a prepared translation. A preliminary accident
report beside a final one. Exit polls beside counts. Provisional cause of death. First-pass
triage beside diagnosis. Live captioning beside a corrected transcript. Preprint beside
published version. A newsroom's first wire beside its correction.

**FALSE MOVE.** Ranking the two — treating the fast output as a defective version of the
accurate one. That is the exact error the seed material was recorded to prevent. Both are
legitimate; the question is whether the system **says which it is holding**.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the system explicitly labels provisional output as
provisional and that downstream users demonstrably treat it that way. Or show no accurate
later version exists, in which case there is no pair and no instrument.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 3 (mediation), Axis 4 (timing), Axis 12 (a record is a
translation).

**MINDS SHARPENED.** PINA primarily; SIIRI where the question is what the record can hold.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR002-05 asks how a delay changes the state being waited on. This asks
about **two simultaneous outputs of different epistemic status**, which is not a delay at
all — both exist, and the problem is that neither is labelled.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A seismic magnitude revised upward days later, after response
decisions were made on the first figure. (2) An election night projection that shapes the
concession before the count.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument; the underlying distinction comes from a reviewed artist
interview already in the library index, and that interview establishes the distinction, not
any fact about a subject.

### PR005-04 — WHICH SENSE IS ALLOWED TO CONFIRM

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: fields have a sensory hierarchy of proof, usually unstated, and things must
often be converted into the privileged sense before they count. Sharpens Axes 2 and 12. No
new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** Every evidential field privileges one sense as the one that settles a
question. Evidence arriving in another sense must be converted — imaged, plotted,
visualised — before it is admitted, and the conversion is treated as neutral when it is not.

**QUESTION.** Which sense is treated as proof in this field, and what has to be converted
into that sense before it is allowed to count?

**CARRIERS.** Auscultation beside imaging in diagnosis. Sonification of astronomical or
particle data. Wine and food sensory panels. Non-destructive testing by ear or touch.
Forensic evidence. Machine condition monitoring by vibration and sound. A field biologist's
ear against an automated survey. Any dataset that is plotted before it is believed.

**FALSE MOVE.** Blindness as metaphor, and the superpower frame — both named in SIIRI's
MUST NOT BECOME. Also: asserting that the privileged sense is *wrong*. The instrument asks
what the **hierarchy** is and what the conversion costs, not which sense is better.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the field admits evidence in multiple sensory forms on equal
terms, with documented cases decided by the non-privileged one.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 2 (perception), Axis 12 (measurement). Reaches Axis 11.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** SIIRI primarily — the instrument's range, and what can become
evidence at all.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR001-07 asks whether something can be registered by a system at all.
This asks a narrower and more concrete question about **which sensory form a field requires
before registration counts as proof** — which is documentable in protocols, standards and
training.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A gravitational-wave detection communicated as audio and its
epistemic status in the field. (2) A gas leak found by smell and the instrument reading
required before anyone acts.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR005-05 — DESCRIPTION IS A DECISION

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: where a description is what survives of an object, whoever wrote it decided
what the object is for every later user. Sharpens Axes 12 and 10. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A description written to stand in for a thing becomes, for everyone
downstream, the thing. The describer's decisions about what was worth mentioning are
invisible in the result and are almost never attributed.

**QUESTION.** Who wrote the description that now stands in for this object, what did they
decide was worth mentioning, and what does the institution treat as the object once the
description is all that is consulted?

**CARRIERS.** Museum and herbarium catalogue records. Archaeological find reports.
Type specimen descriptions. Auction and probate inventories. Police descriptions of persons
and property. Case notes. Product listings and metadata. Any object whose surrogate record
is searched far more often than the object is examined.

**FALSE MOVE.** Sliding into access provision. Audio description and alt text are legitimate
carriers **only** where the subject is the describer's authorship and its consequences — who
decided, what was omitted, what downstream use the omission shaped. An entry whose thesis is
that description provision is inadequate is an access-deficit story and is refused.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the original is routinely consulted alongside the description,
or that describers and their choices are recorded and contested as a matter of course.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 12 (what a record holds); Axis 10 (authorship). Reaches Axis 2.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** SIIRI primarily — description as a produced representation, and
explicit narration as a possible downgrade.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR004-01 asks which channel is treated as the original. This applies
where the original still exists but is **no longer what anyone consults**, and asks who
authored the substitute.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A natural history collection searched entirely through digitised
labels transcribed by non-specialists. (2) A legal precedent known to practitioners through
headnotes rather than judgments.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR005-06 — SILENCE IS NOT ONE THING

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: a record showing nothing may mean absence, refusal, or an instrument with no
way to register the thing, and most systems collapse all three into one value. This is the
"no reading, not zero" distinction in SIIRI's brief, made into a commissioning question.
Sharpens Axis 12. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** Systems record silence as a single value. But nothing-was-there,
someone-declined, and this-instrument-cannot-hear-that are three different states with three
different consequences, and the collapse is usually invisible to everyone downstream.

**QUESTION.** What does this system record as silence or as nothing — and is that absence,
refusal, or something it has no way to register?

**CARRIERS.** Survey non-response. A blank field in an administrative dataset. Archival
gaps. "No comment" in an inquiry record. Null readings in environmental monitoring. Sonar
and radar returns. A quiet period in seismic or acoustic data. Missing years in a
register. Zero-count cells in official statistics.

**FALSE MOVE.** Treating every silence as suppression. Some silence is genuine absence and
some is ordinary refusal. The instrument requires the subject to supply evidence of **which
kind**, and a subject that cannot is not a carrier.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the system distinguishes the states already — separate codes
for declined, not-applicable, not-measured — and that downstream analysis honours them.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 12 primarily; reaches Axis 2 and Axis 13.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** SIIRI primarily — absent versus unregistered, the mind's first
question.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR001-02 asks how a category shapes what an instrument measures. This
asks what the instrument does with **the absence of a value**, which is a different record,
usually stored in a different field, and usually lost first.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) A species absent from a survey because the method could not detect
it, reported identically to a genuine absence. (2) A benefits dataset where "did not attend"
and "was not offered" occupy the same cell.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.

### PR005-07 — WHAT COUNTS AS THE UNMARKED WAY OF SPEAKING

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: every field has a form of speech or writing it treats as neutral and
unremarkable, and departures from it are read as something about the person rather than as
variation. Sharpens Axes 13 and 11. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** A field's "neutral" register is a specific, historically produced form that
someone had to learn. Deviation from it — accent, disfluency, pace, syntax, formality — gets
read as a property of the speaker, typically competence or credibility, rather than as
distance from an arbitrary centre.

**QUESTION.** What does this field treat as an unmarked, neutral way of speaking or writing,
and what is a departure from it read as?

**CARRIERS.** Academic prose conventions and peer review. Broadcast standard pronunciation.
Courtroom testimony and perceived credibility. Clinical history-taking. Code review and
commit message convention. Customer service scripting. Automatic speech recognition training
data and who it fails. Oral examination.

**FALSE MOVE.** Proxy substitution, explicitly forbidden: accent and dialect prejudice is
frequently about class, region or migration, and **those are not disability proxies**. This
instrument is licensed where the subject supplies evidence about how a *communicative form
itself* is being scored — and it must not be used to import another group's situation.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the field has examined and documented its own register
effects, or that outcomes do not vary with the form once content is controlled.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 13 (gaze and social perception); Axis 11 (classification). Reaches
Axis 3.

**MINDS SHARPENED.** Shared — PINA on the channel and its conventions, ZENO on the
threshold at which variation becomes deviation.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR001-09 asks how a name substitutes for an explanation. This is about
**form rather than label**: nothing is being named, and the judgement is made before any
category is applied.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) Speech recognition systems whose word error rate varies by speaker
group, and what the deploying institution concludes about the speakers. (2) A grant panel's
treatment of a proposal written outside its house style.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument. NO PROXIES applies with unusual force here — see FALSE
MOVE.

### PR005-08 — WHICH FORM DID THE FIELD NAME IT AFTER

**STATUS:** **APPROVED_DURABLE** — owner authorisation 2026-09-13.
*Owner reason: a phenomenon that exists in several physical forms usually gets named after
the form one group perceives most readily, and the name then quietly narrows what the field
studies. Sharpens Axes 2 and 12. No new axis.*

**MECHANISM.** Sound is pressure and vibration; it is named for hearing. Light is radiation;
it is named for sight. Where a phenomenon has multiple physical manifestations, the naming
choice encodes a default perceiver, and research questions, instruments and funding follow
the name rather than the phenomenon.

**QUESTION.** What is this phenomenon actually made of, which of its forms did the field
name it after, and what has gone under-studied because the name pointed elsewhere?

**CARRIERS.** Sound as vibration in music, architecture and industry. Infrasound and
seismic coupling. Haptic and tactile transmission of music. "Colour" in remote sensing
outside the visible band. Thermal and chemical signalling in biology. Ultrasound in animal
communication. Any instrument that registers a band the naming sense cannot.

**FALSE MOVE.** The superpower frame, and the compensation myth — that losing one sense
sharpens another. Also: this is **not the acoustics mind**. Sound is one carrier of this
instrument, explicitly not its subject.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Show the field studies the full phenomenon under a general name, or
that the non-named forms are well covered under their own research programmes.

**EXISTING AXIS.** Axis 2 (perception and its range); Axis 12. Reaches Axis 11 (naming).

**MINDS SHARPENED.** SIIRI primarily — a system's account of the world as a representation
produced by an instrument with a range.

**WHAT THIS ADDS.** PR005-04 asks which sense is allowed to confirm a claim. This asks what
the **phenomenon itself** was named for, which is upstream of any evidential hierarchy and
shapes what gets studied rather than what gets believed.

**TRANSFER TEST.** (1) Deaf and hard-of-hearing musicians working with vibration, and what
music research does with that. (2) Elephant infrasound, undetected for decades because the
research question was framed by human audible range.

**BOUNDARY.** Derived instrument — see PROVENANCE above.
