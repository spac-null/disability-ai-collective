## HYPOTHESIS 1

**HYPOTHESIS.** The NOTAM channel assumes a uniform reader: that any pilot,
regardless of how they process dense written information, can and will
correctly triage a mixed stream of coded, undifferentiated, all-caps notices
under real pre-flight time pressure, separating the few relevant items from
the many irrelevant ones. The channel has no mechanism to register
route-relevance or reading-load as a variable — a notice is either issued or
not; whether a given reader can actually extract it in time is entirely
outside what the system measures.

**QUESTION.** Is this assumption caught in writing anywhere specific to this
subject — a formal safety investigation (NTSB, ASRS, an FAA internal review)
that names notice presentation or volume, not system uptime, as the causal
mechanism behind a real missed-notice event, rather than only trade-press
opinion that pilots find the format annoying?

**PREDICTED DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE.** A formal investigation record tying a
specific incident (runway incursion, wrong-runway departure, airspace
violation, missed restriction) to a pilot missing or misreading a NOTAM
because of volume/format — with a stated causal link, not a passing mention.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** If everything found is trade-press or pilot-forum
opinion ("NOTAMs are annoying") with no formal investigation naming
presentation as a causal safety mechanism, the hypothesis stays at the level
of vocabulary and anecdote — Case 4 (THE ACCESSIBILITY FACT) territory, true
but not yet a mechanism — and does not clear the bar.

**INSTRUMENT(S).** PR001-07, "Legibility enables and constrains" (sharpens
Axes 11/12) — closest durable match: what a record's presentation form
permits and forecloses.

**MIND(S) SHARPENED.** SIIRI / ZENO.

## HYPOTHESIS 2

**HYPOTHESIS.** The pre-2026 coded NOTAM format (ICAO Q-codes, heavy
contraction) required every reader to perform a decoding step before content
could be understood — a mediation layer presenting itself as a neutral,
compressed record of the same information while actually changing what
arrives and what it costs to extract it. The FAA's own 2026 replacement makes
"plain-language presentation" its headline feature, which would mean the
agency itself eventually conceded the coded layer was altering, not merely
preserving, what pilots received.

**QUESTION.** Does the FAA's own record — the 2026 announcement, underlying
human-factors work, or congressional testimony behind the 41-year-delayed
replacement — actually attribute past problems to the *coded format itself*
as a translation/mediation problem, as opposed to describing the change in
generic "outdated technology, modernized" terms that would be true of any
legacy IT migration?

**PREDICTED DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE.** A primary FAA/DOT statement or
testimony that specifically names the abbreviation/code format (not the
database architecture) as a safety-relevant translation problem, ideally
with a stated design rationale for adding plain language.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** If the 2026 modernization is framed purely as a
backend/cloud-infrastructure upgrade and "plain-language presentation" turns
out to be a minor marketing line with no documented causal story behind it,
this collapses into Case 2 (COCHLEAR IMPLANT) — a textbook description of
any legacy-to-modern migration, not a subject-specific mechanism.

**INSTRUMENT(S).** PR004-02, "the mediator assigns a register to the
mediated" (Axes 3, 13) — tests whether the translation layer imposes its own
register/interpretation on the content rather than passing it through
unchanged.

**MIND(S) SHARPENED.** PINA.

## HYPOTHESIS 3 — generated, then dropped by the PERSON-ASSUMPTION CHECK

**HYPOTHESIS.** Delta Air Lines had a working backup NOTAM system during the
January 2023 outage and chose not to use it "out of deference to the FAA"
(CEO Ed Bastian, on record). An airline's own working redundancy was
suppressed in favor of centralized regulatory authority.

**QUESTION.** What does this decision assume about who is authorised to act
when a shared channel fails?

This pattern-matches Axis 5 ("independence and assistance") and Axis 10
("participation and authorship") by vocabulary — dependency, authorization,
who is allowed to act.

**PERSON-ASSUMPTION CHECK (v1.1, per
`.claude/perspective-trials/PERSPECTIVE_TRIAL_CONTRACT.md`):** Does this
hypothesis identify an assumption about a *person's* body, perception,
sensory processing, communication, timing, simultaneity, dependence /
assistance, navigation, cognition / capacity, endurance / pace, participation
/ authorship, classification / diagnosis, or normal functioning — or does it
only identify an interesting mechanism about an institution, a process, a
system, or governance?

**Answer: only the latter.** "Deference to the FAA" is a fact about
inter-institutional hierarchy — an airline corporation deferring to its
regulator — not about a body, a perceptual channel, a timing structure, or a
dependence relationship between a person and a thing. Axis 5's own carriers
("a dependency between a person and a device, a route, a schedule or
another person") name a *person's* dependence; Delta-as-corporation is not a
person in the sense the axis means, and nothing here is about who inside
Delta could or couldn't act, or what any individual person's capacity was.
This is the exact pattern Trial 001 caught by hand (institution authored the
terms; institution couldn't verify contents) — a hypothesis that resembles
an axis structurally, using the same words (dependency, authorization),
without naming an assumption about a person.

**DROPPED BEFORE TARGETED RESEARCH.**

## SUMMARY AND SELECTION

Three hypotheses generated. One (H3) is dropped by the PERSON-ASSUMPTION
CHECK before research — an institution-shaped hypothesis (inter-corporate
deference), not a body-shaped one. This is the check working as intended:
v1.1 was added because Trial 001 needed a manual pass to catch two such
candidates after the fact; here the same category of candidate was named
and dropped as a first-class step, not an afterthought.

Two hypotheses (H1, H2) pass the check. Per the trial protocol ("select ONE
strongest for targeted research"), one is chosen to carry into Step 4:

**H1 is selected.** It is the more decisively falsifiable of the two — it
asks for a formal safety-investigation record with a stated causal link,
the strongest tier of carrier the doctrine names, and a clean disconfirming
shape (trade-press opinion alone does not satisfy it). H2 is not
disqualified — it passed the same check and stayed live — but it carries a
real risk of collapsing into Case 2 (COCHLEAR IMPLANT: a true, generic
modernization description that would fit any legacy-to-cloud IT migration)
unless research turns up something FAA-specific and unusual. H2 is recorded
here as a live, unpursued candidate rather than silently discarded, in
keeping with the doctrine's "nothing deleted, nothing rejected without a
record" practice.
