SEED: Christine Sun Kim, *All Day All Night* (Whitney/Walker), specifically
*Screen Real Estate for ASL Interpreters* (2022) — a comparative,
multi-instance measurement of screen space allocated to ASL interpreters
across many real, dated broadcasts, sorted into three institutional
postures (token/self-congratulatory, exiled to a separate feed, or
"we forgot").

TRANSFERABLE QUESTION (frozen before target selection): for a system that
presents two channels as part of one complete communication, does the
actual space/time each is allocated — measured and compared across many
real, dated instances — expose which channel the system treats as primary,
in a way no single instance or stated policy would admit; and does the
pattern sort into token/exile/omission postures rather than a binary?

TARGET: newspaper/broadcast correction practice, measured against
journalism's own stated parity standard (RTDNA: a correction should get
"as much prominence as the error itself had").
