## OBSERVATION FROM THE WORK

*Screen Real Estate for ASL Interpreters* does not simply state that ASL
interpretation is often missing or minimized from broadcast media — it
**measures** it. The drawing is a set of rectangles, each sized to the
actual amount of screen space an ASL interpreter was given during a
specific, named, dated broadcast (a mayoral Twitter post, BLM press
conferences, COVID press conferences). Kim then attaches three recurring
outcomes as parenthetical categories, not a single verdict: "(2–10 SECONDS
OF SELF-CONGRATULATORY FOR BEING ACCESSIBLE)," "(SEPARATE ASL FEED)," and
"('WE FORGOT' OFF-SCREEN)." The mechanism is comparative and cumulative —
no single instance proves anything; the pattern across many dated,
real instances does. The Whitney's own framing states the wider practice
"upends the societal assumption that spoken languages are superior to
those that are signed" — but the specific move in this work is narrower
and sharper than that general claim: it converts a felt, qualitative
experience (being an afterthought) into a **measured, comparable
quantity** (rectangle area) that makes an inconsistency between a stated
commitment (accessibility, inclusion) and an actual, physical allocation
of space legible and undeniable.

## TRANSFERABLE QUESTION

**For a system that presents two channels of information as though both
are part of the same complete communication, does the actual space or
time each channel is allocated — measured and compared across many real,
dated instances, not asserted from a single case — expose which channel
the system treats as primary and which as an afterthought, in a way no
single instance would show and no stated policy would admit; and does
that exposed pattern sort into distinct institutional postures (token
inclusion, exile to a separate channel, outright omission) rather than a
single binary of present/absent?**

## WHY THIS QUESTION COMES FROM THE SEED

It is not a restatement of "provide the accommodation" (Calibration Case 4:
THE ACCESSIBILITY FACT). It is drawn specifically from *how* the work
argues, not just what it depicts: the artist's method is comparative
measurement across dated real instances, converting something usually
described only in felt/anecdotal terms into a legible, cumulative record.
That method — not the ASL content — is what is being transferred.

## DOES IT DUPLICATE AN EXISTING DURABLE INSTRUMENT?

**PARTLY.** This overlaps with PR004-01, "which channel is treated as the
original" (APPROVED_DURABLE, Axes 2, 3, 12), and with Axis 12's general
concept (what a system can register, what it drops). Both already name
that a system can treat one channel as primary and another as derivative.

**What the raw source adds, that the existing abstraction had lost:** a
*method*, not just a category. PR004-01 states the mechanism exists; it
does not say how a researcher would find or prove it. Kim's work supplies
the operative move directly: don't ask "is this channel present or
absent" (a binary, and Case 4's trap); instead **measure and compare the
actual space/time allocated across multiple real, dated instances of the
same kind of event**, and expect the pattern — not any single instance —
to be the evidence. It also supplies a specific three-way taxonomy of
institutional response (token/self-congratulatory presence, exile to a
separate channel, outright omission) sharper than PR004-01's binary
original/derivative framing. This is the kind of addition worth recording
rather than silently reusing PR004-01's language: the axis said an
assumption exists; the seed says how to go and catch it operating, and in
how many distinguishable shapes.

## FALSE MOVE

Reporting that a channel got less space, once, is not the mechanism —
that is Case 4 again, one level up (a measured accessibility fact instead
of an observed one, still just a fact). The mechanism requires the
*comparison across multiple real instances* to be what does the work, and
requires the discrepancy to contradict something the system itself claims
(completeness, parity, transparency) — an asymmetry with no such claim
behind it, or with a stated and legitimate non-hierarchical reason (a
uniform technical constraint, not a priority judgment), does not qualify.

## WHAT WOULD DISCONFIRM TRANSFER

If, for the eventual target, no comparable multi-instance record of
space/time allocation actually exists to measure (so the comparative
method cannot be applied at all); or if a measured discrepancy is found
but is fully explained by a stated, legitimate, uniformly applied
constraint rather than revealing a priority ordering the system's own
claims deny; or if ordinary domain expertise in the target's own field
already does exactly this kind of comparative measurement as routine
practice, with no distinctive contribution from asking the question this
way (v1.2 GENERIC_DOMAIN_KNOWLEDGE / BASELINE_EQUIVALENT).

## BOUNDARY

This is not "the target reminds us of ASL interpretation" and not "the
target is like being Deaf." It is: Kim's practice demonstrated a specific
*evidentiary method* — measured, comparative, multi-instance space/time
allocation as legible proof of priority — applied here to a domain with
no connection to disability, Deaf culture, sign language, Kim herself, or
any accessibility programme. The target subject must supply its own
independent, real carrier for this method; nothing about Kim's own
subject matter is imported as evidence for the target.
