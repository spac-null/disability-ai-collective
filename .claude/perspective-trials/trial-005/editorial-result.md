none of Kim's method and nothing disability-rooted. The seed succeeded at
directing research somewhere baseline's bounded pass never went; it did
not succeed at revealing something the subject's own field hadn't already
found for itself.

## What this trial actually adds, stated plainly — including about the new
mode itself

This is the first trial to test the source-seeded mode end to end, and the
mode did what it was built to test: the seed outperformed cold scanning at
*directing* research (baseline found nothing on this exact target; the
seed's frozen question found real, confirmable material immediately) but
did not outperform ordinary domain expertise at *explaining* the
mechanism, because ordinary domain expertise — once anyone actually
asked — had already explained it, years earlier, on its own terms. That is
a materially different and more precise finding than "GENERIC_DOMAIN_
KNOWLEDGE" meant in Trials 002 or 003, where the classification rested on
ordinary expertise being *capable* of explaining the mechanism, not on
having *already litigated it in public record*. Whether that distinction
(capable-of vs. already-did) should ever matter to how GENERIC_DOMAIN_
KNOWLEDGE is applied is a genuine open question this trial surfaces but
does not answer or propose changing.
