## HYPOTHESIS (the frozen question, applied)

**HYPOTHESIS.** Journalism's own professional ethics standard (RTDNA)
states a correction should receive prominence proportional to the original
error. Comparative, multi-instance measurement — not a single anecdote —
of actual correction placement against the placement of the errors they
correct would show a real, patterned discrepancy: corrections given
consistently and measurably less space and prominence than the claims they
correct, sorting into recognizable postures (a brief, standardized
acknowledgment; relegation to a fixed low-visibility channel regardless of
where the error ran; or effective non-existence for lower-profile errors)
rather than the parity the profession's own standard claims.

**QUESTION.** Has anyone actually measured this — a real dataset or
systematic account comparing correction placement/prominence against
original-error placement across many dated cases, the way Kim's drawing
measured screen space across many dated broadcasts — or is "corrections get
buried" a repeated but unmeasured claim?

**PERSON-ASSUMPTION CHECK (v1.1):** Does this identify an assumption about
a person's communication/perception/access to information, or only an
institutional process (newsroom editorial workflow)? Both are present, as
in Trial 002's NOTAM hypothesis (which also passed v1.1 on this same
basis): the mechanism concerns whether *readers* — people — reliably
receive an accurate correction to something they already read, which is a
communication-axis claim about a person's actual access to information,
not merely an abstract description of newsroom process. **PASSES v1.1**,
on the same reasoning applied to Trial 002's H1. Note for the record:
unlike Kim's own subject, this mechanism (if real) would affect *all*
readers uniformly, not a population with a distinct dependence on a
particular channel — per the owner's standing instruction, that
universality does not by itself disqualify the hypothesis at this gate;
whether the seed contributed something distinctive despite that
universality is precisely what v1.2, after research, has to decide.

**LIBRARY CROSS-CHECK.** Closest existing instrument: PR004-01, "which
channel is treated as the original" (Axes 2, 3, 12) and Axis 12 generally.
As in `seed.md`, this hypothesis is not a fresh axis-territory discovery —
it is the seed's comparative-measurement *method* applied to a target the
existing instrument's language alone would not have pointed at with this
much operational precision (measure real, dated, comparable instances; do
not assert from a single case).

**SELECTED FOR TARGETED RESEARCH** — the only hypothesis this trial
generates by design; the source-seeded mode tests one frozen question
against one target, not a three-candidate sweep.
