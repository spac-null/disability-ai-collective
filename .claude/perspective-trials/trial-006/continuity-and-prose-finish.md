# Trial 006 — Continuity + Prose Finish

Applied together as one surface pass over `article-draft.md`, per
`CONTINUITY_SYSTEM` and `PROSE_FINISH_SYSTEM`
(`automation/new_engine_v1/composition.py`). No new fact, number, date,
name, place, quotation, cause, consequence, or comparison was added. No
qualifier, scope, attribution, or hedge ("may" vs. "does") was dropped.
Same thesis, same order of ideas, same conclusion, same emphasis. Output:
`article-final.md` (938 words).

## What changed (surface only)

- Cut research-report cadence where attribution was already established
  by context ("In PAGER's own published explanation... the USGS states
  plainly" → "PAGER's own published explanation... states plainly";
  "The USGS team's own reasoning" → "Their own reasoning," referent
  unchanged from the immediately preceding paragraph).
- Merged two short, related sentences into one where the seam was doing
  no work ("Green and yellow alerts — the routine, lower-stakes
  majority — go out the moment..." combined without changing content).
- Removed a few instances of editorializing framing adjectives with no
  factual content ("correspondingly blunt," "a little unusual" → "unusual").
- Dropped "simply" from the closing sentence; no claim changed.
- Removed the working-title label and made the headline plain.

## What did not change

Every quotation, date, number, name, and causal/non-causal distinction in
`article-draft.md` — including the explicit disclaimer that PAGER's
account does not tie the "organizations may mobilize" risk to the Van
case specifically — survives into `article-final.md` unaltered in
substance. No fact outside `ledger.md` was introduced at this stage.
