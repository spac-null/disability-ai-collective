SEED QUESTION: When a system produces a fast, real-time output ("the gist
is there, hopefully") and a slower, accurate one ("with the luxury of
time... with accuracy") about the same event, does the system mark which
kind of claim each is — or does whichever arrives first quietly become
"the record," which the slower, accurate one then has to displace rather
than simply complete?

RELATION TO EXISTING DURABLE INSTRUMENT: SHARPENS (Axis 12, Axis 3;
closest instrument PR001-07) — does not duplicate.

WHAT PRIMARY KIM MATERIAL ADDED: The specific, non-obvious framing that a
fast output ("the gist, hopefully") is not a defective version of an
accurate one but a different, equally legitimate epistemic mode — which
is what turns the transferable question into "does the system mark the
difference in kind," not "is fast information less reliable than slow
information" (a truism). This framing was not available from Whitney's
curatorial paraphrase (Trial 005's gap) — it required Kim's own direct
answer to a direct question.

v1.1 RESULT: PASS — a communication/cognition-axis claim about real
decision-makers acting under time pressure, not an abstract institutional
description.

TARGETED RESEARCH: Decisive, and more precise than either predicted
extreme. USGS's PAGER system explicitly states that "demanding or
awaiting... a high level of accuracy may delay response and increase the
loss" — a deliberate design choice to act on fast, openly uncertain
estimates for humanitarian reasons. The predicted failure mode is real
and named: the 2011 M7.2 Van, Turkey earthquake, where an initial "red"
PAGER alert was revised a day later, with published system literature
stating plainly that "organizations may mobilize emergency response based
on initial high alerts that are subsequently downgraded." Following that
and similar cases, USGS added "a review step for critical alerts... to
ensure stability," explicitly weighing "a minor delay" against "the
increased potential for sending out a premature, erroneous alert" — a
real, narrow, evidence-driven compromise neither the "full deferral" nor
"bare exposure" hypothesis predicted in that shape.

v1.2 RESULT: GENERIC_DOMAIN_KNOWLEDGE — disaster-response science already
identified and engineered around this exact tension through its own
methods (a real documented case, published system-design literature),
independent of Kim, the seed, or any of the three method lenses.

COLLISION CONTRIBUTION: NEW EVIDENCE DIRECTION — the three-way
disagreement specifically directed research to check PAGER's own
stability-review history rather than stopping at its general uncertainty
language; no single method, and no cold baseline pass, produced that
specific research direction.
