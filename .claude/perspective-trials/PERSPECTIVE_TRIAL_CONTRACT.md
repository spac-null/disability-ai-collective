PERSPECTIVE TRIAL CONTRACT

v1
Trial 001: Svalbard Global Seed Vault.
Observed failure: institution-only hypotheses could survive initial
Perspective hypothesis generation and reach targeted research on the strength
of an axis's structural shape, without a forced check that the hypothesis
actually names an assumption about a person.

v1.1 (owner-approved after Trial 001)
Added PERSON-ASSUMPTION CHECK: before any Perspective-generated hypothesis
may proceed to targeted research, answer whether the hypothesis identifies an
assumption about a person's body, perception, sensory processing,
communication, timing, simultaneity, dependence/assistance, navigation,
cognition/capacity, endurance/pace, participation/authorship,
classification/diagnosis, or normal functioning — or whether it only
identifies an interesting mechanism about an institution, process, system,
governance, infrastructure, classification of non-person objects, or generic
organizational behaviour. If it is only the latter: drop before targeted
research.

Boundary: this check applies to the PERSPECTIVE QUESTION, not the subject.
Crip Minds subjects remain unrestricted (a building, a measurement system, an
airport, a technology, a seed vault, a communications standard, an
institution, an ordinary object). It must not become "the article must be
about disability" or "the subject must explicitly concern a human body." A
generic institutional mechanism is not sufficient merely because it resembles
an axis structurally — the disability-rooted instrument must reveal an
assumption about how people are expected to perceive, act, communicate,
move, endure, depend, participate, or be classified.

Scope: trial-layer hypothesis-selection checkpoint only. Does not modify the
16 durable instruments, the 14 axes, PR001–PR004 statuses, CURRENT_ENGINE,
the live Lens Probe, Worth, Safety, Grounding, or production prompts.

No other change.
