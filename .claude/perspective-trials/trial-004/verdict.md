TARGETED RESEARCH: H1 was killed, not merely unconfirmed. An individualized

10-year-old children on foot died — investigators are quoted stating
plainly that no one caught in the wave's path "ever had a chance to
escape," and the source's own summary line is categorical: "Running in it
was impossible." The evidence directly contradicts the differential-
capacity mechanism H1 proposed, rather than simply failing to confirm it.

WHAT IT ADDED: A small, real correction to how this event is usually told.
Several retrospective accounts foreground the youngest (10) and oldest (78,
65) victims' ages in a way that can imply, without asserting, that age or
physical capacity shaped who died. The primary material this trial actually
checked says the opposite for this specific mechanism: escape was
categorically impossible for whoever the wave reached, regardless of age,
fitness, or available mobility aid (a horse and wagon didn't help). That is
a genuine, if minor, correction — not a Crip Minds angle, but not nothing.
