# Trial 001 — Editorial result

## WHERE THIS STOPPED

Stopped after Step 5 (choosing the surviving reading). Neither the baseline Lens Probe nor
the Perspective Library produced a hypothesis that survived contact with real evidence.
Per the trial's own rule, that is where development stops: no frozen ledger was built, Worth
was never run, and no article was drafted, because there was nothing to hand Worth that
named a mechanism about a body.

## THE ACTUAL RESULT, IN FULL

**Baseline (Step 2).** The current live Lens Probe, run exactly as it exists against the
Crop Trust FAQ anchor and four already-collected sources, answered `"assumption": "none"`.
This is the probe's own designated correct answer for a subject that assumes nothing about
anybody — not a failure of the probe, and not a weak effort; the governance and engineering
material in front of it does not commit to a claim about a person's body, perception,
timing, dependence, or classification, and the probe is built to say so rather than invent
one.

**Perspective Library (Step 3).** Working from all 14 axes and the 16 approved instruments
independently, three candidates were generated:

1. *Who authored the vault's operating terms* (Axis 10 / PR003-08) — a real governance
   mechanism, disqualified before research because it names an assumption about an
   *institution*, not a body.
2. *The vault cannot verify what it stores* (Axis 12) — a real epistemic mechanism, same
   disqualification: the unregistered thing is a box of seeds, not a person.
3. *The tunnel's design assumes something about who moves through it* (Axis 7/1) — the one
   candidate that, if supported, would have cleared the bodily-mechanism bar. Sent to real
   research.

**Targeted research (Step 4).** Candidate 3 found a genuine, sourced, subject-specific
design decision: the entrance tunnel was built sloping *downward* into the mountain, against
local coal-mine convention of sloping *upward* for drainage, because a downward slope was
easier to build. That decision is the documented mechanical cause of the 2016–17 meltwater
incident that forced the ~$13M repair. It is a strong particular. It is not about a body —
it is about permafrost, gravity, and a construction-convenience decision colliding with a
warming climate a decade later.

**Surviving reading (Step 5).** Neither survives as a Crip Minds reading. The tunnel-slope
finding is real and specific enough to be a legitimate piece for a different kind of
publication — an infrastructure or climate-engineering story about a design shortcut that
looked practical in 2008 and became the facility's single point of failure by 2016. In this
publication's own vocabulary (`WORTH_SYSTEM`), that shape is
`GREAT_GENERAL_STORY_WRONG_PUBLICATION`: a real story, for somewhere else. The governance
material (black-box custody, unilateral rule-setting) never rose even to that level — it
stayed a fair general-interest institutional detail, not a story carrier on its own.

## WHAT WAS NOT DONE, AND WHY

No frozen ledger was built (Step 6's LEDGER step), because Worth only has standing to
evaluate a ledger against a claimed lens, and there was no lens left to evaluate. Running the
ledger-and-Worth machinery on a subject that has already, honestly, produced no candidate
would be theatre: it would either manufacture a mechanism to feed it, which is exactly the
failure this whole doctrine exists to refuse, or run Worth against an empty claim and get a
HOLD that this trial had already reached by hand. WORTH is recorded as NOT_RUN rather than
simulated to a predetermined HOLD.

No article draft exists. There is nothing after this record to read as prose; the honest
retained output of this trial *is* this record.
