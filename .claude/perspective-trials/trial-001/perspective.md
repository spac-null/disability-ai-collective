# Trial 001 — Perspective Library application

Applied independently of the baseline result above, using only: the 14 axes in
`.claude/crip-minds-perspective-doctrine.md` §2, the 16 APPROVED_DURABLE instruments listed
in `.claude/perspective-research/INDEX.md`, the approved axis sharpenings, and the core
rules (WORLD FIRST, NO PROXIES, accessibility fact ≠ insight, hypothesis must be able to
fail, NOTHING HERE is valid). No candidate entries, no M1–M10 worked verbatim, no roleplay.

## METHOD NOTE

The subject was read axis by axis. Most axes returned nothing on contact — the material in
`subject.md` is an ownership/governance/engineering story with no fact about a person's
body, perception, timing, dependence, or classification. Three axes produced something
worth writing down as a candidate. Two were disqualified before research on a category
check explained below; the third was sent to real research.

## CANDIDATE 1

**HYPOTHESIS.** Norway/NordGen, as sole legal owner and operator, unilaterally sets every
term a depositing genebank operates under (the GM-seed ban, the -18°C standard, the
black-box handling rule, no direct access to one's own box without a formal request) —
depositors do not co-author these terms; they are admitted into conditions someone else
settled.

**QUESTION.** Did any depositor genuinely co-author the vault's operating terms, or were
all of them admitted into terms Norway alone set?

**PREDICTED DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE.** A governing document, treaty text, or advisory-council
record showing depositor genebanks held a formal vote or veto over operating rules (would
weaken this reading), versus a record showing Norway/NordGen set the rules alone and
depositors could only accept or decline participation (would support it).

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Evidence that depositor institutions, or an international body
representing them, co-designed the operating rules; or that Norway's own authorship was
itself constrained by a treaty obligation depositors had negotiated.

**INSTRUMENT(S).** PR003-08, "who defines the option set" (APPROVED_DURABLE).

**MIND(S) SHARPENED.** MAYA / ZENO.

**WHY THIS WAS NOT SENT TO RESEARCH.** Axis 10 in the doctrine is explicitly framed as a
question about who authored the conditions *a body has to live inside* — the doctrine's own
worked example is a workplace built and occupied before an employee had any say in its
design. Applied here, "depositor genebank" is an institution, not a body. Nothing in this
candidate names what a person is assumed to be, perceive, or depend on; it names what an
*institution* is permitted to decide. That is a real governance mechanism and a fair general
story, but running it as a Crip Minds candidate would be doing exactly what the doctrine
warns against: borrowing the *shape* of an axis (authorship, admission into settled
conditions) without its actual subject (a body). This is caught at the category check, before
any search — no fact would rescue it, because the hypothesis was never about a body to begin
with.

## CANDIDATE 2

**HYPOTHESIS.** The "black box" rule means NordGen/Norway structurally cannot verify the
contents, condition, or viability of anything inside a sealed depositor box — the vault's
entire promise (a durable backup) rests on trusting an unverifiable, unregistered thing.

**QUESTION.** Does the vault's own documentation acknowledge what it cannot know about a
box's contents or condition, and has that gap ever mattered in practice — for instance, in
anything unusual noted about the condition of the ICARDA boxes on withdrawal?

**PREDICTED DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE.** A statement from the vault, NordGen, or a depositor
about verification limits; or a documented case where black-box trust was tested (e.g., a
box found compromised, mislabeled, or degraded on withdrawal).

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** Evidence the vault does verify samples in some way (e.g., a
viability check on receipt) that would undercut "structurally blind"; or a statement that
the ICARDA withdrawal revealed no problem of this kind at all. (A statement, not silence —
per Axis 12's own false-move rule, "inferring absence from silence" is a fabrication, not a
finding.)

**INSTRUMENT(S).** No exact APPROVED_DURABLE match; closest raw territory is Axis 12
(measurement and what a system can register), adjacent to PR001-07 "legibility enables and
constrains."

**MIND(S) SHARPENED.** SIIRI.

**WHY THIS WAS NOT SENT TO RESEARCH.** Same category failure as Candidate 1, one level
removed. "What a system cannot register" is disability-relevant in the doctrine only when
what it cannot register is something about a *person* — a body, a form of variation, a life a
record has no field for. Here the unregistered thing is a sealed box of seeds. It is a real
and interesting epistemic mechanism (an institution whose central promise depends on
something it can never check), and it would make a legitimate piece about trust,
verification and institutional design — but disability is not "in" it under any reading this
trial could construct without inventing a body for the mechanism to be about. Refused here
rather than reached for.

## CANDIDATE 3 — the one sent to research

**HYPOTHESIS.** The facility's entrance tunnel was built with a specific, documented,
subject-specific design decision — and that decision, or one like it, might reveal what kind
of body the facility assumed would move through it (a navigation/Axis-7-shaped particular),
the way the McGonigle house's own account of its flexibility carries Axis-1/Axis-14 readings
elsewhere in this doctrine.

**QUESTION.** Is there a documented, subject-specific fact about who can physically enter or
move through the vault — a stated design decision about the tunnel's gradient, steps, doors,
or who is permitted inside — and does that decision commit the institution to an assumption
about bodies, the way Axis 7 predicts a route always does?

**PREDICTED DISCRIMINATING EVIDENCE.** An architectural or engineering account of the tunnel
and portal building that states *why* it was built the way it was, in a way that names an
assumption about who moves through it — analogous to the McGonigle architect's own account of
why the house is flexible.

**DISCONFIRMING SHAPE.** No design decision on record names an assumption about bodies at
all — only an assumption about something else entirely (e.g., construction convenience, or
soil/permafrost stability), which would mean the axis fires on the wrong target: a real
design-assumption particular exists, but it is not a body-assumption.

**INSTRUMENT(S).** No exact APPROVED_DURABLE match; raw Axis 7 (navigation and spatial
dependence), tested against Axis 1's general "every system assumes an implicit person"
frame.

**MIND(S) SHARPENED.** MAYA (primarily; navigation is MAYA's secondary gravity) / SIIRI.

**WHY THIS WAS SENT TO RESEARCH.** Unlike Candidates 1 and 2, this hypothesis, if it found
support, would be a genuine bodily particular in the doctrine's own sense — a documented
design decision that commits the institution to an assumption about who can physically use
the facility. It was the only one of the three where a fact, if found, would actually clear
the category bar the other two failed before research began. See `evidence.md` for the
result.
