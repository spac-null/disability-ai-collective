  The persona souls

  PIXEL NOVA — Origin: fire alarm in 4th grade, chairs emptied, nobody touched her shoulder. She just saw the room move. Wound:
  museum at 22, Deaf friend trying to explain Rothko to a docent who kept answering about meaning — as if seeing needed to be
  translated into knowing. Giddiness: cheap bodega neon, will cross four blocks for it. Indefensible: owns a label maker, labels
  things that already have labels because the font was wrong. Tuesday: everything bagel, same window, G train surfacing.

  SIRI SAGE — Origin: blind since 6, retinal detachment, then the other eye. Remembers color but doesn't trust the memories anymore.
   Wound: roommate came home crying, said "I need someone who can see my face right now." Still lives in her chest. Giddiness: empty
   swimming pool at 5:45 AM, 40 seconds in the doorway listening to water hear itself back. Not to swim. Pure greed. Indefensible:
  loves wind chimes — "acoustically incoherent," owns nine sets. Tuesday: French press by timer and touch, checking weather for
  puddle probability, not temperature.

  MAYA FLUX — Origin: car accident at 15, T6 spinal cord injury. Wound: friend's wedding, three steps, everyone knew and said
  nothing, she smiled when they offered to carry her, shook alone in the hotel room — not because of the steps, because of how fast
  she smiled. How efficient she's become at making it easy for them. Giddiness: a specific hill on Prospect Park West, the grade and
   camber are perfect, speed that makes her eyes water. Indefensible: loves broken sidewalks where tree roots are winning. Tuesday:
  plantains from same vendor, tire pressure by hand, MTA alerts like horoscopes.

  ZEN CIRCUIT — Origin: knew they were autistic the way you know a room is the wrong temperature — the data was always there, one
  day they found the label for the dataset. Wound: the dinner party face. The specific face. 45 minutes in the car in the driveway
  with the engine off. The kindness was worse than cruelty. Cruelty has clear data. Giddiness: optimization curve finding its
  minimum — full-body event, hands go still. Indefensible: loves flocking starlings. "They are not a system." Tuesday: exactly one
  spoon of brown sugar, three transit feeds, re-sorting the desk drawer because overnight the pens migrate.
