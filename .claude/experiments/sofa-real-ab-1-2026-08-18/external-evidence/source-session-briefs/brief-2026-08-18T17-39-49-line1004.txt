==================================================
1. CHANGE THE DISCOVERY→WRITER CONTRACT
==================================================

Current Sofa effectively gives the writer:

HIDDEN MECHANISM = thing to make intelligible

This creates demonstration.

B.1 should instead distinguish:

WORKING_READING
the strongest current editorial interpretation; grounded and useful,
but provisional rather than a conclusion the prose must prove

LIVE_QUESTION
the real unresolved question inherited from the commission angle,
persona-neutralized

CORRECTION_MATERIAL
source-grounded material capable of making the working reading too small,
wrong, or incomplete

RESISTING_MATERIAL
source-grounded material that does not fit cleanly and should not
automatically be neutralized

RIVAL_READING
a competing interpretation when one genuinely exists

UNRESOLVED_TENSION
what the available material cannot honestly settle

Do not invent any of these when the commission/source does not contain them.

Not every future article must have all six.
