==================================================
CORRECTION 1 — ATTENTION SIGNAL
==================================================

Use a more observational attention signal.

Do NOT say:

"verbs of physical searching"

Use something functionally equivalent to:

"The review repeatedly describes discovering the festival through verbs
such as walk, follow your nose, sniff out, uncover and figure out."

No access conclusion.
No body conclusion.
No mechanism.

==================================================
CORRECTION 2 — OPEN QUESTION
==================================================

Remove:

"after there was no longer anyone to ask how they wanted to be found"

The source does not establish anything about how these artists wanted to
be found and that clause introduces a new consent/agency thesis.

Use:

"What happens to the festival's language of wandering and stumbling when
it is placed beside artists whose work went unseen until after their
deaths?"

This is the actual blind-writer test.

==================================================
WRITER INSTRUCTION
==================================================

Keep the prose instruction short.

Something functionally equivalent to:

"Write a narrative nonfiction article from the supplied source material.

Follow what the material actually gives you. The question is genuinely
open; do not decide its answer in advance.

If a later fact changes the significance of an earlier one, let that
change happen without announcing the editorial operation.

Do not invent scenes, biography, motives, access barriers, distances,
reporting actions or lived experience.

Do not introduce explicit disability examples merely because this is a
disability-led publication.

Stop when the strongest source-grounded understanding has arrived."

Do NOT add back a long rule-heavy Sofa prompt.
