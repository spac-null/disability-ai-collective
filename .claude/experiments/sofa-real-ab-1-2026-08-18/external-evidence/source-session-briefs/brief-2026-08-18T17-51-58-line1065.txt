==================================================
1. DISCOVERY MAY KEEP THE INTERNAL STRUCTURE
==================================================

Internally, B.2 Discovery may still know:

- provisional reading
- source material that disturbs it
- resisting material
- rival interpretation
- unresolved tension
- grounding boundaries

Do NOT delete that reasoning.

But do NOT expose these to the writer as six prominently labelled
conceptual operations.

The writer must not feel as if it is filling:

WORKING READING
→ CORRECTION
→ RESISTANCE
→ RIVAL
→ UNRESOLVED TENSION

==================================================
2. BUILD A SMALL WRITER-FACING STORY BRIEF
==================================================

Convert the internal B.2 Discovery state into a compact,
persona-neutral writer brief.

For this Edinburgh case, the writer-facing brief should communicate no
more than something functionally equivalent to:

EDITORIAL DIRECTION:
Begin with the festival's own language of walking / following your nose /
discovering hidden artists.

IMPORTANT MATERIAL:
Sandra George materially complicates a simple physical-access reading.
Follow what that changes rather than forcing the original interpretation
to survive.

RESISTANCE:
The review simultaneously demands clarity and enjoys work it describes as
indecipherable/obtuse. This may matter, but only use it if it genuinely
advances the changed understanding.

STOP CONDITION:
Once the strongest source-grounded changed understanding has landed,
stop. Do not add another disability example, second world, analogy, or
larger theory merely to keep the essay going.

Do NOT use these exact sentences automatically.
Construct the brief from the real frozen commission/source.

The brief is editorial guidance, not article prose.

==================================================
3. GIVE THE WRITER MATERIAL, NOT LABELS
==================================================

The writer should receive:

- source snapshot
- source anchor
- a short editorial direction
- selected carrying material in useful order
- correction/resisting source excerpts
- concise editorial notes separated from evidence
- known gaps / grounding boundaries

Do NOT prominently expose terms like:

working_reading
live_question
correction_material
resisting_material
rival_reading
unresolved_tension

in the prose-generation prompt.

Those may remain internal field names in code if useful, but the generated
writer prompt should not teach the model to narrate those operations.

==================================================
4. DO NOT SCRIPT THE TURN
==================================================

Do not tell the writer:

"place the correction before midpoint"

or:

"now introduce the resisting example"

or:

"state the live question"

Instead:

"Allow later material to change an earlier reading when it genuinely does.
Do not announce the operation. Let the new fact create the turn."

The movement should feel caused by the world.

==================================================
5. PERSPECTIVE
==================================================

No persona roleplay.

No invented autobiographical first person.

No:

"I was told at nineteen..."
"I know this from inside..."
"I keep returning to a Deaf man..."

First person only if there is a REAL recorded reporting/editorial action in
the evidence record.

Otherwise use ordinary narrative nonfiction without an invented narrator.

The disability lens remains causally necessary but may remain narratively
implicit.
