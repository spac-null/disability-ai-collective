Follow Your Nose: The Uneasy Pleasures of Edinburgh Art Festival

You can't walk into a gallery at the Edinburgh art festival without learning about some subcultural genius or long-lost creative you've never heard of. That's the whole point of the thing, really — a city-wide celebration of the underground and the unheralded. Queer narratives, secret histories, undiscovered archives. By the time you're done you'll have uncovered so many hidden stories you'll feel like Columbo.

But EAF is also a big, unwieldy, confusing thing. Curated displays, satellite exhibitions, performances, off-site projects — it's impossible to figure out how any of it fits together. You've just got to follow your nose and hope you sniff out something you like. And you'd better like photography, because there's a lot of it here.
