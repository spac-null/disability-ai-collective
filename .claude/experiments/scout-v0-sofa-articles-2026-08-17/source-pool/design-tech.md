# Source Pool — Design/Architecture + Technology Reporting

Category: design/architecture reporting (architecture criticism/trade press, urban design,
product design, infrastructure engineering) AND technology reporting (tech industry news,
software/hardware trade press, standards-body documents).

Excluded per brief: anything about disability, accessibility, wheelchairs, Deaf/blind/
deafblind, neurodivergence, autism, "inclusion," "universal design," "assistive tech,"
"accessible UX." Banned phrases avoided.

Below: every real source actually fetched/reviewed during this search, with retrieval
status and whether it produced a disturbance card.

## Sources reviewed but not carded (weak fragment, credibility concern, or redundant)

  Repair" (https://3v.org/nyc-sidewalk-sheds-and-local-law-11-why-the-shed-is-cheaper-than-the-repair/,
  Aug 15, 2026). Fetched; good numbers, but the site's own network (self-described
  sister properties: "Media Presser, Policy Maker, Opinion, OSINT, K4I") reads like a
  low-provenance content network rather than an editorially accountable outlet. Used
  only to cross-check, not as the cited source — The Hustle's independently-reported
  version (with a named councilmember quote) was used instead.

- **AI Home Building** — "An Algorithm Scored Your Office Building 81% Ready for
  Apartments. It Didn't Ask Where the Sunlight Goes."
  (https://aihomebuilding.com/articles/ai-office-residential-conversion-algorithm-limits,
  Apr 18, 2026). Strong disturbance shape (Gensler's "Conversions+" feasibility score
  vs. IRC light/air code, San Francisco's exposure-requirement waiver for office-to-
  residential conversions producing legally windowless bedrooms) but the outlet itself
  — 610+ articles, generic-sounding byline roster, sensational headline pattern — could
  not be verified as an editorially accountable publication on inspection of its own
  homepage. Not carded; flagged here in case the underlying facts (Gensler Conversions+
  tool, SF adaptive-reuse ordinance Section 140 exposure waiver, 55 Broad Street
  conversion) are worth re-verifying via a primary source (Gensler press materials,
  SF Planning Dept ordinance text, or a mainstream trade outlet like Bloomberg CityLab
  or The Real Deal) for a future pass.

- **Allen Matkins legal alert** — "Repurposing San Francisco Office Buildings: Pending
  State and Local Laws" (https://www.allenmatkins.com/real-ideas/repurposing-san-francisco-office-buildings-pending-state-and-local-laws.html,
  Apr 21, 2023). Confirms SF's adaptive-reuse ordinance exempts conversions from
  "residential dwelling unit exposure requirements" among others, but is dated (2023,

  kept as a lead for the office-conversion story above.

  Independent Repair," "The End of Parts Pairing? Almost.," "Apple's Parts Pairing

  (not directly fetched); corroborates The Register piece's framing of Apple's 2024

- **LEED performance-gap research** (Forbes 2014 opinion piece, ScienceDirect paper,

- **Desire paths / pedestrian-path data** (Forbes, 99% Invisible, Southern Urbanism
