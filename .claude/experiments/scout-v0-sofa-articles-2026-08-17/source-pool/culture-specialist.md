# Source Pool — Culture Reporting & Small/Specialist Publications

Category: culture reporting (music, visual art, film, literature, museums,
festivals) AND small/specialist publications (niche newsletters, hobbyist
trade zines, regional culture magazines, industry-specific specialist
outlets). Explicitly excludes disability, accessibility, wheelchairs,
Deaf/blind/deafblind, neurodivergence, autism, and "inclusion" topics, and
avoids the banned example phrases from another brief.

Sources below were located via independent web search and read (WebFetch) in
full or substantial part — not invented. Roughly a dozen search threads
pursued across museums/natural history collections, music reissue
newsletters, sign-painting/quilting trade coverage, philately, film archives,
folklore/oral-history blogs, and awards-rules journalism; three real sources
produced disturbance-shaped fragments strong enough to card.

## 4. Hidden female portrait beneath Picasso's Blue Period "Portrait of Mateu Fernández de Soto"
- **Outlet**: The Courtauld Institute of Art (news/blog)
- **URL**: https://courtauld.ac.uk/news-blogs/2025/hidden-picasso-portrait-discovered-beneath-important-painting-from-his-blue-period/
- **Date**: February 10, 2025
- **Field**: visual art / conservation imaging / provenance
- **Note**: Reviewed in full. Contains a real discrepancy (the painting was
  catalogued as a portrait of "an unknown woodcarver" for decades before
  being correctly identified as Picasso's friend Mateu Fernández de Soto,
  and a second, still-unidentifiable sitter has now been found beneath
  that), but the piece frames this straightforwardly as an exciting
  discovery rather than leaving an unresolved procedural tension — thinner
  disturbance shape than the three cards above. Kept as a candidate, not
  carded.
- **Status**: reviewed, no card (framing too resolved / conventional "hidden painting" story)
