## QUICK PERCEPTUAL PROBES (compact, not full engine-before-persona experiment)

### Candidate: Camera trap detection failure (ecology)
- Pixel Nova — WHAT DOES THIS NOTICE: the record and the event are not the same category
  of thing; absence-in-data is not proof of absence-in-reality, and the gap between them is
  patterned, not random. WHAT ASSUMPTION BECOMES QUESTIONABLE: that a wildlife "rarity"
  ranking describes populations rather than partly describing which populations a given
  recording technology is bad at holding. WHAT COULD THE OBJECT BECOME: a camera trap
  becomes an instance of a general problem — any device built to preserve a fleeting thing
  has a blind spot shaped like the thing it's worst at holding. EVIDENCE NEEDED: the paper's
  own numbers (already in hand), plus one further real case of instrument bias correlated
  with its target variable, from a different domain, to widen the mechanism.
- Zen Circuit — notices the same detection-probability-by-body-mass table as a pattern/
  threshold problem (does the model correct for it, or treat detection as constant?), but
  has less to say about the mediation/transmission angle specifically; would produce a more
  procedural, less felt piece.
- Siri Sage — notices the sensory-hierarchy angle (a camera "sees" some animals and not
  others the way sighted design privileges some senses), but the source material is about
  a machine's mediation of animal presence, not about lived sensory experience directly —
  a weaker, more inferential fit than Pixel's.
- Verdict: Pixel Nova, clearly. Assigned.

### Candidate: xAI Memphis "non-road engine" turbines
- Zen Circuit — WHAT DOES THIS NOTICE: a classification satisfied by a superficial formal
  feature (wheels) that has no correlation with the substantive variable regulators
  actually care about (continuous stationary operation, real emissions). WHAT ASSUMPTION
  BECOMES QUESTIONABLE: that "the category was met" and "the thing the category exists to
  track is present/absent" are the same claim. WHAT COULD THE OBJECT BECOME: a turbine on
  wheels becomes one instance of a general administrative pathology — any classification
  scheme checkable by form can be satisfied without tracking function, and once satisfied,
  unlocks a completely different regime. EVIDENCE NEEDED: EPA's own January 2026 finding
  (in hand), the literal regulatory text distinguishing mobile/non-road from stationary
  sources, and real reporting on the Memphis neighborhood's documented health/environmental
  context to ground scale (not to invent one).
- Pixel Nova — notices the timing angle (turbines relocated/re-designated before the
  364-day clock), which is real but secondary to the form-vs-function mismatch that is the
  disturbance's core; a plausible but less load-bearing fit than Zen's classification
  engine.
- Maya Flux — notices the infrastructure/environmental-justice angle (who breathes the
  consequence), real and worth keeping present in reporting, but her core engine is
  friction/route/promise-vs-delivery in the built environment, not classification logic
  itself.
- Verdict: Zen Circuit, clearly. Assigned.

### Candidate: ACV insurance labor depreciation
- Maya Flux — WHAT DOES THIS NOTICE: a formula borrowed wholesale from one domain
  (physical material wear) applied to a domain with no matching physical property (labor
  performed today), producing a number that looks actuarially rigorous while functioning as
  a one-directional value transfer. WHAT ASSUMPTION BECOMES QUESTIONABLE: that "actual cash
  value" is a stable, technical, apolitical calculation rather than a live and reversible
  political settlement (Alaska adopted a ban, then rescinded it in nine months with no
  change in the underlying economics). WHAT COULD THE OBJECT BECOME: a home insurance claim
  becomes an instance of a general pattern — costs get "depreciated" by borrowing a
  physical metaphor that doesn't actually apply, wherever a payer benefits from the
  borrowed metaphor holding. EVIDENCE NEEDED: the Michigan bulletin, Alaska's adoption and
  reversal, the court split (already in hand); further research into what actually drove
  Alaska's reversal, if discoverable.
- Zen Circuit — notices the classification-instability angle (is labor "tangible" or not,
  and why did the category flip within nine months), a real but secondary read; Maya's
  cost-literacy engine is the sharper, more causally load-bearing fit for a piece about
  who absorbs a manufactured accounting fiction.
- Pixel Nova — no strong fit; nothing about mediation/translation/timing is the load-
  bearing mechanism here.
- Verdict: Maya Flux, clearly. Assigned.

## FINAL THREE

1. **Camera trap detection bias** → Pixel Nova
2. **xAI Memphis turbine classification** → Zen Circuit
3. **ACV insurance labor depreciation** → Maya Flux

Chosen together because they are three genuinely different disturbance shapes (instrument
blind spot correlated with its object; category satisfied by form not function; a
borrowed-domain formula applied where its underlying property doesn't exist) — not the
same conceptual trick three times — and three different world objects (a research paper
about wildlife cameras; a live EPA enforcement fight over data-center power; an insurance
regulatory flip-flop), none reused from AR1/AR2/AR3's sources, none of the four banned
example searches, and none selected for an obvious disability angle. Siri Sage was
considered for all three and genuinely lost each probe — not omitted by rotation rule,
which this V0 explicitly does not apply.
