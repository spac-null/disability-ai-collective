# Article Form Transfer Evaluation

Judged against the **Test-2 Form** (`ARTICLE-FORM.md`), not FORM-1.3.
Article: `run/article.md`, "Bell or Horn", 1,587 words.

## A. Does the movement arise from the Staniforth Road material?

**Yes.**

Observed motion, paragraph by paragraph:

| Para | Movement | Function |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the place, concretely — fence, gradients, three signs, painted words, tactile paving, brick, ballast, cobbles | premise |
| 2 | the report's own safety-case sentence, quoted, uncommented | premise closes on the two channels |
| 3–6 | the board; the undefined rule; bell-or-horn as the driver's choice; the training document repeating the same two words; RAIB's own observation of drivers choosing differently; the recorder logging both on one channel | **narrowing** |
| 7–10 | the shift, the runners, the run itself, the accident in seconds | event |

| 12 | Morden Hall Park 2008 and Saughton 2018 — warning given, people died | **resistance** |

| 14 | the remedy line: board, recommendation, "could have addressed" | **recurrence** |
| 15 | the arrival | **arrival, terminal** |

That is narrows → accumulates → recurs, which is what this Form specified and what the
material supports. The order is the Form's order. Nothing was reordered to fit a shape.

One genuine Form deviation, in the writer's favour: the Form put the runners' background in
movement 5 (arrival). The article moved it to movement 3, where the run is introduced, and
carried only the operative clause forward to the landing. That is a natural equivalent, not
a departure — it avoids explaining who they were at the moment of arrival.

## B. Did it revert to Edinburgh geometry?

**No.**

- **No semantic two-meanings arrival.** The landing is not that a word means two things.
  It is that two channels named by one conjunction are different *kinds of thing* — one a
  property of a place, one a sent message. That is a structural claim about a system, not a
  claim about vocabulary. The word "or" is quoted, but the piece does not argue that "or"
  has a second meaning; it argues that the two things it joins are not commensurable.
- **No countervoice.** Nothing in the piece speaks against the reading. There is no
  equivalent of the reviewer's conviction pressing on a forming argument. The resistance is
  evidential (paragraph 12), not rhetorical.
- **No correction of a speaker.** RAIB is never corrected, contradicted, or shown to have
  missed something. The piece uses RAIB's findings throughout and simply follows them
  further than the report's own remit goes.
- **No forgotten or marginalised figure**, no historical non-showing, no present-encounter
  versus historical-visibility hinge.

## C. Does the 2008/2018 material genuinely disturb, or merely decorate?

**Genuinely disturbs.**

By paragraph 11 the reader has been handed a complete and satisfying explanation: the
recorder logged no bell, RAIB says probably none was sounded, the horn came too late.
Paragraph 12 arrives immediately on that and gives two cases where the warning **was**
given — sounded, and repeatedly sounded — and people died anyway. The easy reading cannot
survive it, and the piece does not tell the reader that. It states the two cases plainly and
moves on. That is the resistance doing structural work: it removes the available conclusion
without supplying a replacement, which is what forces the accumulation and the arrival to be
necessary rather than ornamental.

Test: delete paragraph 12 and the article still runs, but the landing becomes unearned —
it would read as "the driver should have rung the bell, and here is a fancy way of saying
so." The paragraph is load-bearing.

## D. Is the arrival about the system, or about blame?

**System.**

The arrival is a property of the warning channel: its ordinary state is silence; silence at
this crossing does not distinguish an empty track from an unsent message; and a channel that
is nearly always silent, and nearly always silent truthfully, trains the people who use it.
No individual is blamed. The driver is not faulted — the piece keeps "probably" and never
offers a reason. The runners are not faulted; the report's own observation that they did not
look is stated once, in the event sequence, as fact, and is not returned to at the landing.
The operator's rules are described, not condemned. No sign, no decision, no person carries
the arrival.

## E. Does it arrive once and stop?

**Yes.** The final paragraph is the only statement of the landing. It is not restated, not
paraphrased, not widened to trams in general, other senses, other crossings, or society.
There is no closing paragraph after it, no recommendation, no call to action, and no
"that is the distinction" construction. The last sentence is the arrival.

## F. Is the perceptual instrument necessary but invisible?

**Necessary: yes. Visible: no.**

- **Necessary.** Nothing in the article is legible as an argument without the instrument.
  RAIB supplies every fact used, including the discretion, the single recorder channel, and
  the prior cases; what RAIB does not do is treat hearing as a channel that can simply be
  off. The piece's entire move — separating a property of a place from a sent message, and
  then asking what silence on that channel can mean — is a deafness-derived way of reading,
  and no other reading of this evidence produces the arrival.
- **Invisible.** The words deaf, disability, access, hearing loss, and impairment do not
  appear. No disabled person appears. Neither pedestrian is characterised as deaf or hard of
  hearing. The lens is never announced, never explained, and never given a name. It is
  present only as the article's way of looking.

Verified by search: the article contains no occurrence of "deaf", "disab", "access",
"impair", "inclusion", or "barrier-free".

## Additional Form observations (not part of A–F)

**Length overrun — the one clear defect.** The Form specified "roughly 900–1,200 words …
Do not pad. If the arrival has genuinely landed at 900 words, stop there." The article is
**1,587 words**, about 32% above the ceiling. This is a real failure of a stated Form
control, and it is the only one.

Diagnosis: the overrun is concentrated in movements 3 and 4 — the event sequence
(paragraphs 7–11, ~430 words) and the prior-occurrence block (paragraphs 12–14, ~430 words).
Both are places where the Form asked for many specific facts in a fixed order, and the writer
rendered each as its own clause rather than compressing. Paragraph 10 in particular narrates
the shift, the runners' background, and the approach separately when the Form's movement 3
asked only for "the accident itself, plainly and chronologically, in seconds."

It is a *local* defect: it does not distort the movement, does not add a second arrival, does
not introduce unsupported material, and does not change what the piece concludes. It is
length control failing on a material type that supplies far more enumerable fact than
Edinburgh did — the Form carried the shape across but not the compression.

**Prompt-level inaccuracy the writer corrected.** The frozen system prompt's landing
paragraph says the two pedestrians "had crossed this tramway fewer than ten times". The
source says they had completed the *circuit* fewer than ten times, and that the circuit
crosses the tramway twice. The article writes "on every previous circuit those two runners
had made — fewer than ten of them", which is faithful to the source rather than to the
prompt. Recorded as a note on the packet, not as a defect in the article.

## VERDICT

**LOCAL_DEFECT**

The architecture transferred. The Form produced by Discovery + Article Form was the right
shape for this material, the article followed it, the resistance did structural work, the
arrival is systemic and terminal, and the instrument stayed necessary and invisible. One
clearly isolated Form control — length — failed, in a way that is diagnosable to a specific
cause and does not affect the transfer question.

Not PASS, because a stated Form control demonstrably did not hold.
Not FAIL, because none of the failure modes defined before execution occurred: no Form
reversion, no inertness, no instrument collapse, no remedy drift.
