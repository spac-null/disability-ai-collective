WHERE THE MEANING CHANGES
  Read the list again with that in mind and 'the eight most interesting' stops being a claim about design and becomes a claim about what a camera can hold. The room made of salt was chosen by a process that could not perceive most of it - and this is what it is like to be assessed through a channel that does not carry you.
  The idea that does this work: A festival travels in the medium it is documented in, and that medium quietly decides which work can travel at all: when the medium is the photograph, a design whose content is smell, weight or the temperature of a wall arrives with its content removed, and is then ranked against work that photographs well.
