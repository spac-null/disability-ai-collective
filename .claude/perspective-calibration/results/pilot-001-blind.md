### VERSION X

**HYPOTHESIS:** The estimate rests on a classification that sorts coffee by country-assigned group rather than by lot — origins are placed in variety and quality groupings that then anchor indicator prices. Once a country sits in a group, the group becomes the explanation for what its coffee is worth: the assignment describes the crop and is then used as the reason the crop prices where it does. A farm that changes what it grows or how it processes is, at this level, invisible until the country is reassigned — and there may be no stated route by which reassignment is requested, evidenced or appealed.

**EVIDENCE PROBE:** The published group definitions and the country list attached to each, with the dates of any changes; the governance minutes or rules specifying who approves a change; the presence or absence of a documented request or appeal mechanism; and whether recent reports use group membership causally ('because it is a Robusta origin') rather than descriptively.

## P04

### VERSION X

**HYPOTHESIS:** The dummy is an instrument that converts a crash into a small set of numbers (head injury criterion, chest deflection, femur load) and everything a body can suffer that has no channel on this device is absent from the safety record by construction, not by oversight. The load-bearing move is that the agency's own documents state these limits — biofidelity evaluations and rulemaking notices typically say plainly which body regions the device does not measure and which injury mechanisms it cannot register. A vehicle can therefore be fully compliant with respect to injuries the instrument has a sensor for, and silent on the rest, while the compliance statement reads as a statement about occupant safety generally.

**EVIDENCE PROBE:** NHTSA biofidelity and injury-criteria technical reports, and Federal Register preamble text for the 1986 incorporation and later amendments, where the agency states instrumentation limits and regions without injury criteria; the injury-criteria table in the standard, whose listed measures define the denominator of 'safe'.
