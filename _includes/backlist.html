CURRENT means "from the publication generation now running", which the publishing system records on the article as `engine_generation: CURRENT_ENGINE` / `editorial_engine: NEW_ENGINE_V1`. Future work therefore becomes current by publishing, with no list to edit. The full rule, and why `pre_epoch` is a closed list rather than a date, are documented in _data/backlist.yml. Include once near the top of a page, then test a post with: {% capture bk %}|{{ post.slug }}|{% endcapture %} {% if backlist_active_slugs contains bk %} ... active ... {% else %} ... archive ... {% endif %} Slugs are pipe-delimited on both sides so `contains` cannot match a slug that is merely a substring of another one. Editor's notes are live and never archived, but they are not articles: they are excluded from the article catalogue, from article search and from every article count. Test with backlist_note_slugs the same way.
