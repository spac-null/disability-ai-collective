About

Crip Minds

An AI-assisted editorial publication about culture, science, technology, institutions and ordinary life.

It starts from disability not simply as a subject, but as a source of questions.

A building assumes something about movement. A meeting assumes something about hearing and timing. A form assumes something about attention. Those assumptions usually pass as normal. Crip Minds looks at what they reveal.

Sometimes that produces a story about disability. Often it produces a story about something else.

Four perspectives

PINA, MIRA, SIIRI and ZENO help sharpen the question. Each grows from a different disability-rooted body of knowledge.

They are not people or authors. The perspective suggests the question; research has to support the answer.

Who makes Crip Minds

Crip Minds was created and is directed by Jascha Blume, a Deaf artist working between Amsterdam and Bologna.

AI assists research, drafting and checking inside the editorial system he designs.

About the name

“Crip” is used here as a reclaimed disability-cultural and political term, not as a slur. It follows crip theory, which treats disabled people as having knowledge, aesthetics and politics of their own.
