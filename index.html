What this is

Not writing about disability — seeing the world through it.

Crip Minds is an experimental editorial publication by Deaf artist Jascha Blume. It can begin with a mycelial network, a deep-sea ecosystem, a cut in a film, or a bottle of water on a supermarket shelf — then ask what a disabled way of seeing reveals about how the thing itself works.

The source provides the floor. The way of seeing provides the leap.
— Jascha Blume, editor →
