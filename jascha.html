Personal statement

Jascha Blume

Editor, Crip Minds  ·  Amsterdam / Bologna

I am a Deaf artist, born and raised in Amsterdam and now based between Amsterdam and Bologna. Nederlandse Gebarentaal, Dutch Sign Language, is my mother tongue.

At the Gerrit Rietveld Academie, interpreters gave me access to spoken teaching. They also made a structure visible: translated information arrives through another person and on another timetable. In a group, that timing can affect when you can respond and join the exchange.

That experience became material for De Gebarentaaltolk en Ik. The work examines interpretation and back-translation through synchronized videos. The versions remain connected, but they do not become identical.

Video became an important medium in my work because sign language exists through movement, sequence, space, and facial expression. Static sign notation cannot hold all of that information.

Retrieve My Time (2010) also returned to time and access: what it means to catch up with something that was not available at the moment it happened.

Deaf gatherings can create a temporary sense of homeland: a place where sign language and visual attention organize the room from the start, rather than being added afterward.

The same difference appears in ordinary communication. A telephone number may technically provide contact, but phone-only contact is not usable access for me. Written communication changes whether participation is possible in practice.

In Bologna, my work with L’Altro Spazio treated accessibility as a starting point for a cultural place, not a correction added after the important decisions had been made.

Crip Minds continues these questions. A disabled way of experiencing reality is not a deficit to explain. It can reveal something about a system, object, or institution that the dominant account has failed to notice.

I built the publication as an authored system. I design its fictional editorial voices, source rules, factual boundaries, and ways of testing an idea. AI works inside those choices; it does not replace their authorship or turn a constructed persona into a real person.

A phrase behind this work is iets terughalen wat eigenlijk van mij is, maar op afstand van me is komen te staan: recovering something that is mine but has come to stand at a distance from me. Another word for it is terugeisen — reclaiming.

Signo ergo sum. I sign, therefore I exist.
