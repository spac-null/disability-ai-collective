Editorial perspectives

Crip Minds uses four disability-rooted perspectives to ask better questions. They are not authors or representative characters; they are forms of attention.

PINA
Relation, mediation, simultaneity.
MIRA
Dependence, infrastructure, maintenance, hidden cost.
SIIRI
Perception, legibility, nonvisual knowledge.
ZENO
Pattern, classification, normality.

Perspective generates the question. Research establishes the facts.
