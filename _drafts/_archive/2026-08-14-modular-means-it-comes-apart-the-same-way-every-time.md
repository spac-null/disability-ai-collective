The word doing the most work in UCLA's wildfire housing project, presented at [Dezeen School Shows](https://www.dezeen.com/school-shows/) (an annual exhibition of student work from leading design schools worldwide), is "modular." A module repeats. That is the whole point of a module. You draw one unit, you certify it once, and then you stamp it out across a burned hillside as fast as the trucks arrive.

I keep circling one question. When you certify a repeating unit once, what exactly are you certifying into permanence?

Start with the mechanism. An evacuation route that assumes everyone can walk over debris excludes people before anyone draws a module. The person who uses a power wheelchair does not get down the street. This is not a design flaw discovered afterward. It is the built environment doing precisely what it was drawn to do.

So the recovery draws a module. [Nathan Logan's "Medium" project](https://www.nathan-logan.com/), from the [Future Histories of the City studio at UCLA](https://www.aud.ucla.edu/), describes "chains of mini-towers" that "delicately emerge from the ground." Delicate. Emerge. Lovely verbs. A stair does not emerge from the ground for someone who cannot climb it. It simply stands there and excludes.

My first draft said the architects forgot disabled people. Then I read the school statement again. They wrote that they "interrogate contemporary urban issues" with "equal measures of expertise, optimism and vision." Nobody forgot anything. Optimism is a design input. It selects for the future resident who arrives whole, and it does this on purpose, because a module optimized for the hardest evacuation would cost more per unit.

Speed saves lives. A family in a tent in February does not benefit from a bespoke unit. One that ships in 2028 arrives too late. Faster and cheaper and repeatable is not cruelty. It is triage. I believe that. I also believe triage drafted once and stamped out runs long after the emergency ends. The module that housed the fire survivor becomes the housing stock for forty years. Its stairs sort the next generation too.

A rule can be drafted once, in a hurry, by people who meant well, and left running with nobody present to feel bad about it.

The street excludes by accident. The module will do it on schedule, at scale, with a certificate.
