How It Works

How Crip Minds Works

{{ site.creator }} — cripminds.com — 2026

01 — The subject

Crip Minds begins with a factual subject and a tension inside it: the detail that does not quite fit the obvious explanation. The subject needs no connection to disability, and most have none.

The central question is not “how does this affect disabled people?” It is: what does a disabled way of seeing make knowable about this subject that the usual framing has missed? The best answer changes what the subject appears to be doing.

02 — The perspective

One of four disability-rooted editorial perspectives helps generate a sharper question — PINA, MIRA, SIIRI or ZENO. Each is a knowledge lineage: a body of disability knowledge from which a particular kind of attention has been developed. They are not people, have no biographies, and are never an article’s author.

A perspective is not evidence. It may decide which question gets asked first. It cannot establish that anything is true, and no perspective owns a subject.

03 — The research

Sources are then gathered specifically for that article: the piece that prompted it, and the further documents fetched and read alongside it. The reading has to survive that material, not just the first page of it. Everything the article treats as fact comes from here.

04 — The argument

The article may interpret beyond what any individual source explicitly concludes. That is the point of doing the reading rather than summarising it.

What it may not do is invent the ground it stands on. Names, dates, numbers, quotations and events must be traceable to a source. Unsupported material is cut or marked as uncertain.

05 — The check

Before publication the article is checked for factual support, for whether its central claims survive their sources, and for fidelity to what those sources actually say. Quotations and factual claims are checked against live sources.

An article that does not survive those checks is held, not softened. There is no fixed publishing rhythm, and nothing publishes to fill a slot.

06 — Authorship

{{ site.creator }} designs the editorial system, the perspectives, the constraints, the source rules and the publication standards. AI-assisted research, drafting and checking run inside that design.

So articles are credited to {{ site.article_credit }}, the publication, rather than to a person as if they had been written and line-edited by hand. That is the accurate description of how they are made. The exact internal mechanics are not part of the public write-up; the system report goes further into what runs in production and what it will not do.
